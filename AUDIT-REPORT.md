# Fact-Check Audit — Change Report

*Audit of every document in `My-Hero-Academia-2.0` against the manga (ch. 1–431), the anime (ep. 1–171), official reference books, and reference reporting (ANN, Wikipedia, My Hero Academia Wiki, behind-the-scenes interviews).*

All errors found were corrected **in place**. This file is the log of what was wrong and what it now says.

---

## Summary

- **All 19 Markdown documents** were re-read and checked.
- Roughly **110 factual corrections** were made across plot chronology, character fates, episode/chapter citations, Quirk mechanics, voice credits, publication data, and the franchise chronology.
- No prose was cut for length; the documents retain their structure.
- The tier-list counts (96 boys + 40 girls = **136**) were independently re-counted and confirmed correct.

## Major factual errors (highest priority)

### The Hood fight was placed in the wrong season
- *02-seasons/season-5.md* and *03-characters/pro-heroes.md* and *03-characters/league-of-villains.md* described the **Endeavor vs. High-End Nomu Hood** fight as happening during Season 5 (eps. 103–106) in Jaku City, with Midoriya, Bakugo and Shoto present.
- **Correct:** Hood is fought in **Season 4, episodes 87–88** ("Japanese Hero Billboard Chart" / "His Start"), immediately after the chart ceremony, in a **Kyushu city**; Endeavor and Hawks fight it (with the Flaming Sidekickers nearby) — the three students are not present. Dabi sent Hood; Mirko debuts afterward. The Season 5 work-study episodes (102–103) instead cover **Starservant** and **Ending** (who kidnaps Natsuo). Episodes 89–90 (Season 5) close the Pro Hero Arc with Dabi's aftermath and the first vestige dream.

### Stain did not kill Native, and did not escape custody
- *season-2.md* said Stain kills Native in the alley and later escapes custody.
- **Correct:** Native survives the alley (he is saved; he only dies years later in the Final War, at Jaku). Stain is imprisoned in **Tartarus** immediately; he does not escape until the mass Tartarus breakout during Season 6.

### Bakugo's hero name
- *season-2.md* / *class-1-a.md* said **Dynamight** was rejected at the naming ceremony.
- **Correct:** Bakugo proposed **King Explosion Murder**, then **Lord Explosion Murder**; Midnight rejected both. He kept his final name secret until he gave **Great Explosion Murder God: Dynamight** to Best Jeanist during the Paranormal Liberation War (**manga ch. 293**).

### When Bakugo learns One For All
- *class-1-a.md* placed it "at the end of Season 2"; *season-2.md* ended episode 38 with Bakugo confronting Midoriya.
- **Correct:** the truth comes out in **Season 3, episodes 60–61 ("Deku vs. Kacchan, Part 2") at Ground Beta**; All Might explains everything and apologises. Episode 38 ("Encounter") is Midoriya's mall meeting with Shigaraki.

### Iida does not take the name Ingenium at the naming ceremony
- *season-2.md* hero-name table had him choosing **Ingenium** in episode 26.
- **Correct:** in episode 26 Tensei (hospitalised) tells him not to rush, so Tenya registers his real name; he first declares himself **Ingenium** in the field while facing Stain (eps. 29–30).

### Tenko Shimura's family
- *season-5.md* said Tenko killed his mother, his "two sisters," and his father.
- **Correct:** Tenko had **one older sister, Hana**; their mother was **Nao**, and Nao's parents **Chizuo and Mako Magi** (maternal grandparents) lived in the house. The corgi **Mon** died first, then Hana, then Nao and the grandparents, then Kotaro deliberately. All For One engineered the tragedy (and supplied the destructive Quirk), then took him after every adult on the street ignored him.

### Mr. Compress's arm and lineage timing
- The implication that he lost an arm during the Meta Liberation Army arc was wrong.
- **Correct:** his **left arm is destroyed by Overhaul** during the prisoner-transport ambush (he retaliates by compressing Overhaul's arms off). His **Oji Harima lineage reveal** and self-mutilation to free the League happen in the **Paranormal Liberation War** (Season 6), not the MLA arc.

### Chisaki's fusions
- Tier/season text said Overhaul fused "with the compound and Mimic."
- **Correct:** he fuses with his subordinate **Shin Nemoto**, and later **Rikiya Katsukame**, plus the environment. Mimic only merges with the labyrinth separately.

### Curious's killer and the PLF roster
- Season 5 listed "Mr. Compress vs. Curious" and listed **Curious as a PLF lieutenant**.
- **Correct:** **Himiko Toga** fights and kills Curious at Deika City (awakening Transform); **Dabi** is Geten's opponent. Curious is dead by the time the Paranormal Liberation Front is formed and cannot be a lieutenant (the field commanders are Re-Destro, Geten, Trumpet, Skeptic, Slidin' Go plus the League regiment leaders).

### Aizawa's injuries were wildly misattributed
- *season-3.md* / *ua-staff.md* said the Vanguard Action Squad "broke his arms, burned him and hospitalised him for weeks"; one line even credited Moonfish to Aizawa.
- **Correct:** in the forest Aizawa efficiently neutralises dozens of low-tier villains and clashes with Dabi twice (one is a Twice clone), taking only minor damage. Moonfish is fought by Bakugo, Todoroki and the berserk Dark Shadow. His broken arm/face injuries happened back at the **U.S.J. (Season 1)** at the Nomu's hands. His right-leg amputation and right-eye damage occur in the **Paranormal Liberation War (Season 6)**; afterward he retains only limited Erasure use through his remaining eye.

### Joint Training results and Round 1 score
- One file had Round 1 as **4–1 with Kaminari captured**; others had Honenuki "caught anyway."
- **Correct:** Round 1 ends **1-A 4–2** — 1-B's Kuroiro/Shiozaki capture **Koda and Kirishima**; Kaminari lets himself be taken as a decoy and is freed when Shinso brainwashes Shiozaki. Round 3 is a **1–1 draw**: Iida captures Kaibara, Pony captures Todoroki; Honenuki ends the match unconscious, un-captured, under his own collapsed tower.

### Toga's death and Uraraka's awakening chapters
- *tier-list-girls.md* placed both at **chapter 428** and described Toga fatally stabbing Uraraka.
- **Correct:** the conclusion is **chapter 395** (anime ep. 169, "The Girl Who Loves Smiles"); Toga transforms into Uraraka and gives her own blood to transfuse her, dying in the process. Uraraka's mass-Gravity awakening is across chapters 393–395.

### Kirishima's backstory episode
- Class/season text dated the timid-middle-school flashback to Season 3 (eps. 39–40).
- **Correct:** it plays during the Rappa fight in **Season 4, episode 72 ("Red Riot")**.

### Recipro Burst duration
- *class-1-a.md* called Recipro Burst a "three-minute" sprint.
- **Correct:** Recipro Burst lasts **ten seconds** (engines stall afterward); **Recipro Turbo** (Joint Training, ep. 95) runs for **ten minutes**.
