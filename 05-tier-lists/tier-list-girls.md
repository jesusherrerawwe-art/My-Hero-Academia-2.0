# Power Tier List — Girls

*All female characters ranked at their peak power across the full series. Heroes, students, staff, and villains ranked together.*

> **⚠️ Spoiler warning:** This list includes events from the manga's final arcs and the anime's final season. See the [methodology](README.md) for how rankings were determined.

## S+ — Reality-Altering

Characters whose abilities operate outside the normal rules of the series.

---

### 1. Star and Stripe — *New Order*

**Justification.** The No. 1 Hero of the United States and, by the series' own statement, the strongest hero in the world.

**New Order** is the most powerful Quirk ever shown. By touching a target and speaking their name, Star and Stripe imposes a **rule that becomes reality** for that person — "if you touch me, you die," "your heart stops," "you cannot move." The rules do not need to be physically plausible. She can also apply rules to herself, granting flight and enormous strength.

She is the only character who nearly kills an awakened Shigaraki outright, and she does it while he is at his most complete. She loses only because All For One exploits the mechanism of New Order itself — the requirement to know and speak a name — rather than by overpowering her.

The limitation that keeps her at S+ rather than above it: New Order requires **touch**, a **known name**, and it can only impose a limited number of rules at once. A character who avoids all three conditions cannot be affected.

---

### 2. Eri — *Rewind*

**Justification — special case.** Rewind reverses a living body to a previous state. At full, uncontrolled output it does not merely heal or de-age: it can **rewind a person past their own birth and erase them from existence.**

Eri ranks this high because of her Quirk's absolute ceiling, not her ability to use it. She is a small child who cannot control the power, who activates it only in extremis, and who spent most of the series terrified of her own body. When she does activate it — on Midoriya during the Shie Hassaikai raid — she reverses catastrophic injuries instantly and nearly erases him entirely in the process.

**This is a ceiling ranking, not a combat ranking.** In any conventional confrontation Eri loses to almost everyone on this list. She is placed here because no other Quirk in the series has a higher theoretical maximum.

## S — Catastrophic

Among the strongest beings in the world.

---

### 3. Mirko — *Rabbit*

**Justification.** The most relentless combatant in the entire series, male or female.

Mirko fights exclusively with her legs — she has no ranged options, no defence Quirk, and no regeneration. During the Paranormal Liberation War she engages **multiple High-End Nomu simultaneously**, loses an arm and most of her remaining limbs, and **keeps fighting**. She is still conscious and still attacking when she is carried off the battlefield.

Her ranking reflects two things: raw physical output, which is genuinely S-tier, and a durability-and-will combination that no other character matches. She is the clearest answer to the question of whether a purely physical fighter can compete with Quirk-heavy opponents.

---

### 4. Himiko Toga — *Transform*

**Justification.** Toga drinks a person's blood and assumes their appearance and Quirk. At her peak she is not limited to one form: after drinking **Twice's** blood she gains **Double**, allowing her to create perfect copies of anyone she has transformed into — including copies of powerful heroes, in unlimited numbers.

She is also a genuinely lethal close-quarters fighter in her own right, using knives and her own body with complete disregard for injury.

Toga ranks at S for realised threat rather than raw output. A single Toga is an A-tier combatant; a Toga with Double and a supply of blood is an army.

---

### 5. Nana Shimura — *Float / One For All*

**Justification.** The seventh wielder of One For All and All Might's mentor. She possessed **Float** in addition to the accumulated stockpile, and fought All For One directly during the era when he controlled Japan's underworld.

She ranks at the top of S rather than in S+ because she was ultimately killed by All For One, and because the stockpile she carried was smaller than the version All Might and Midoriya later inherited.

Her legacy, however, is the structural hinge of the entire series: her decision to abandon her family to protect them from All For One is what produced **Tomura Shigaraki**.

## A — Elite

| Character | Quirk | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| **Mt. Lady** | Gigantification | A fixed 20.6-metre form with proportional strength. At her peak she helps restrain **Gigantomachia**, which almost nothing else in the series can do. |
| **Ryukyu** | Dragon | Full dragon transformation. Carried and threw a High-End Nomu, and leads Pro Hero squads in the Paranormal Liberation War. |
| **Nejire Hado** | Wave Motion | Spiral shockwaves with genuine piercing force and flight. Her sustained output lets her compete with ranked Pro Heroes. |
| **Lady Nagant** | Rifle | Her right arm becomes a rifle firing bullets she can curve mid-flight. She hits Midoriya's heart from kilometres away — the most precise long-range attack in the series. |
| **Thirteen** | Black Hole | Creates miniature black holes that disintegrate anything. Offensively the ceiling is effectively unlimited; Thirteen is ranked A rather than S because they are a rescue specialist who avoids combat and were incapacitated early. |
| **Momo Yaoyorozu** | Creation | Can create any non-living object whose molecular structure she understands. The ceiling is bounded only by her knowledge and lipid reserves, and at her peak she commands squads of Pro Heroes. |

## Observations

**The female cast is smaller but has a higher ceiling.** Star and Stripe's **New Order** is the most powerful Quirk in the entire series, and **Eri's Rewind** has the highest theoretical maximum of any ability ever shown. Neither of these has a male equivalent.

**Mirko is the outlier.** She has no Quirk-based defence, no ranged attack, and no regeneration — and she still ranks third, purely on physical output and refusal to stop. She is the strongest argument in the series that willpower functions as a power scaling mechanic.

**Support Quirks cluster at the top.** Six of the eleven A-and-above characters have Quirks that are as useful for rescue and logistics as for combat: Zero Gravity, Creation, Black Hole, Dragon, Wave Motion, and Rifle. This reflects a deliberate choice by Horikoshi — the strongest women in the series are generally the most versatile, not the most destructive.

**Thirteen is the clearest mis-ranking.** Black Hole can disintegrate anything it touches, including S-tier opponents. Thirteen is placed at A because they chose a rescue career and avoid using it offensively — a reminder that these tiers measure realised combat application, not theoretical lethality.
