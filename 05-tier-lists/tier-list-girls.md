# Power Tier List — Girls

*All female characters ranked at their peak power across the full series. Heroes, students, staff and villains ranked together.*

> ⚠️ **Spoiler warning.** This list is built on the manga's final arcs and the anime's final season, including character deaths and Quirk awakenings. See the [methodology](README.md) for how rankings were determined.

**40 characters ranked.** The summary table and the detail sections below are identical — use whichever is easier to read.

## S+ — Reality-Altering

*Abilities that operate outside the normal rules of the series.*

### 1. Star and Stripe (Cathleen Bate) — *New Order*

The No. 1 Hero of the United States and, by the series' own statement, **the strongest hero in the world.**

**New Order** is the most powerful Quirk ever shown. By touching a target and speaking their name, she imposes a **rule that becomes reality** for that person: *"if you touch me, you die," "your heart stops," "you cannot move."* The rules do not need to be physically plausible. She can also impose rules on herself, granting flight and enormous strength.

She is the only character who nearly kills an awakened Shigaraki outright, and she does it while he is at his most complete. She loses because All For One exploits the *mechanism* of New Order — the requirement to know and speak a name — rather than by overpowering her.

What keeps her at S+ rather than above it: New Order requires **touch**, a **known name**, and can only hold a limited number of rules at once. A character who avoids all three conditions cannot be affected.

⚠️ She dies, and All For One steals New Order. **In her last moments she sabotages the Quirk from within**, making it rebel against the rest of Shigaraki's power set and setting his body's completion back by a week — which is the only reason the heroes have time to prepare for the final battle. She wins after she is dead.

### 2. Eri — *Rewind* (ceiling case)

Rewind reverses a living body to a previous state. At full, uncontrolled output it does not merely heal or de-age: it can **rewind a person past their own birth and erase them from existence.**

Eri ranks here because of her Quirk's absolute ceiling, not her ability to use it. She is a small child who cannot control the power, who activates it only in extremis, and who spent most of the series terrified of her own body. When she does activate it — on Midoriya during the Shie Hassaikai raid — she reverses catastrophic injuries instantly and nearly erases him in the process, which is what makes **Infinite 100%** possible at all.

**This is a ceiling ranking, not a combat ranking.** In any conventional confrontation Eri loses to almost everyone on this list. She is placed here because no other Quirk in the series has a higher theoretical maximum — and because ⚠️ she eventually masters it well enough to **restore Mirio Togata's lost Permeation**, which nothing else in the world could do.

## S — Catastrophic

*Among the strongest beings in the world.*

### 3. Mirko — *Rabbit*

The most relentless combatant in the entire series, of either list.

Mirko fights exclusively with her legs. She has no ranged option, no defensive Quirk and no regeneration. ⚠️ During the Paranormal Liberation War she engages **multiple High-End Nomu simultaneously**, loses her left arm and most of her right leg, and **keeps fighting** — she is still conscious and still attacking when she is carried off the battlefield. In the final war she returns on prosthetics and is on the team assigned to Shigaraki.

Her ranking reflects two separate things: raw physical output, which is genuinely S-tier, and a durability-and-will combination nothing else in the series matches. She is the clearest answer the story offers to the question of whether a purely physical fighter can compete with Quirk-heavy opponents.

### 4. Himiko Toga — *Transform*

Toga drinks a person's blood and assumes their appearance **and their Quirk.** At her peak she is not limited to one form: after drinking **Twice's** blood — collected by Dabi during the war — she gains **Double**, and can produce perfect copies of anyone she has transformed into, in number.

⚠️ In the final war she does exactly that: transformed into Twice, she floods multiple battlefields with clones of herself, overwhelming Pro Heroes who are otherwise out of her league. She is also a genuinely lethal close-quarters fighter in her own right, using knives with complete disregard for her own injuries.

Toga ranks at S for realised threat rather than raw output. A single Toga is an A-tier combatant; a Toga with Double and a supply of blood is an army.

⚠️ She dies in **chapter 395** (adapted in episode 169, *"The Girl Who Loves Smiles"*). After Uraraka reaches her and accepts her for who she is, Toga transforms into Uraraka and **gives her own blood to transfuse Uraraka**, who is dying from blood loss, dying in the process.

### 5. Nana Shimura — *Float + One For All*

The seventh wielder of One For All and All Might's mentor. She carried **Float** in addition to the accumulated stockpile, and fought All For One directly during the era when he controlled Japan's underworld.

She ranks at the bottom of S rather than in S+ because she was ultimately killed by All For One, and because the stockpile she carried was far smaller than the version All Might and later Midoriya inherited.

Her legacy is the structural hinge of the entire series: her decision to abandon her family to protect them from All For One is what produced **Tomura Shigaraki.** The strongest villain in the story exists because the fifth-strongest woman on this list tried to keep her son safe.

|---|---|---|
| **Mt. Lady** | Gigantification | A fixed 20.6-metre form with proportional strength and mass. ⚠️ She physically attempts to stop **Gigantomachia** during his crossing, which almost nothing else in the series tries. Sizable, durable, and impossible to ignore on a battlefield. |
| **Ryukyu** | Dragon | Full dragon transformation with flight and enormous lifting capacity. Leads Pro Hero squads in the Paranormal Liberation War and is one of the few heroes who stands when Shigaraki arrives. |
| **Nejire Hado** | Wave Motion | Spiral shockwaves with genuine piercing force, plus flight. Her sustained output lets her compete with ranked Pro Heroes, and she is on the team assigned to Shigaraki in the final war. ⚠️ She finishes at **No. 7 on the Hero Billboard Chart JP** eight years later. |
| **Lady Nagant** | Rifle (+ Air Walk) | Her right arm extends into a rifle with an integral scope, and she moulds her **hair** into any bullet she wants — including rounds that curve in flight. | **Thirteen** | Black Hole | Creates miniature black holes that disintegrate anything they touch, regardless of durability. Offensively the ceiling is effectively unlimited. | **Momo Yaoyorozu** | Creation | Can create any non-living object whose molecular structure she understands, limited only by knowledge and lipid reserves. ⚠️ She designs and leads the operation that finally puts **Gigantomachia** to sleep — the single most consequential tactical plan any student executes in the series — and commands Pro Hero squads in the war. |
| **Ochaco Uraraka** | Zero Gravity | Nullifies gravity in anything she touches, and combines it with **Gunhead Martial Arts** for close combat. ⚠️ During the final war (chapters 393–395) her Quirk **awakens**: she extends Zero Gravity across an entire swarm of Toga's Doubles at once, stopping all of them in mid-air over the battlefield — an area-effect feat no other student in the series matches. |

## Observations

**The female cast is smaller but has a higher ceiling.** **New Order** is the most powerful Quirk in the entire series and **Rewind** has the highest theoretical maximum of any ability ever shown. Neither has a male equivalent — the strongest two abilities in *My Hero Academia* both belong to women on this list.

**Mirko is the outlier.** No Quirk-based defence, no ranged attack, no regeneration — and she still ranks third, purely on physical output and refusal to stop. She is the strongest evidence in the series that willpower functions as an actual power-scaling mechanic, not just a theme.

**Two of the top five win by giving something away.** Star and Stripe sabotages her own stolen Quirk from beyond the grave; Toga spends the last of her blood saving the girl trying to stop her. Both are ranked for what they *did*, and in both cases what they did was not an attack.

**Support and utility Quirks cluster unusually high.** Zero Gravity, Creation, Black Hole, Dragon, Wave Motion, Search and Rifle are all as useful for rescue and logistics as for combat. That reflects a deliberate choice by Horikoshi: the strongest women in the series are generally the most *versatile*, not the most destructive.

**Thirteen is the clearest deliberate mis-ranking on this list.** Black Hole disintegrates anything it touches, including S-tier opponents, and Thirteen is placed at A because they chose a rescue career and refuse to use it offensively. These tiers measure realised combat application, not theoretical lethality — and Thirteen is the character who proves the difference matters.
