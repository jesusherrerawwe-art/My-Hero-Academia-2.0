# Power Tier List — Boys

*All male characters ranked at their peak power across the full series. Heroes, students, staff and villains ranked together.*

> ⚠️ **Spoiler warning.** This list is built on the manga's final arcs and the anime's final season, including character deaths and Quirk awakenings. See the [methodology](README.md) for how rankings were determined.

**96 characters ranked.** The summary table and the detail sections below are identical — use whichever is easier to read.

## S+ — Existential

*Capable of ending a city, a country, or the existing social order alone. Cannot be countered by conventional means.*

### 1. Tomura Shigaraki — *Decay + All For One*

At his peak Shigaraki is the most dangerous being the series produces. His awakened **Decay** no longer needs sustained five-finger contact: it spreads outward from anything he touches, propagating through ground, buildings and air in a chain reaction that consumes a city district at a time. Layered on top is **All For One** itself — centuries of stolen Quirks, near-total regeneration, Radio Waves, Search, and a body rebuilt by Doctor Garaki specifically to survive its own output.

He is fast enough to evade every top-ranked hero at once, durable enough to survive Endeavor's Prominence Burn and Mirko's assaults, and his offence is effectively unblockable: contact with him is destruction. The only characters who ever seriously threaten him are Midoriya and **Star and Stripe** — and Star and Stripe dies doing it.

He ranks above All For One because awakened Decay is a more absolute offensive tool than anything All For One ever held in his own body. ⚠️ He dies in the final war.

### 2. All For One

A century of accumulated Quirks, near-immortality through stolen life-extension abilities, and the power to take or impose any Quirk on anyone. He has fought and injured every generation of One For All's wielders, engineered the birth of his own successor, and lost only twice — to prime All Might, and to a fully realised Midoriya.

His true peak is the version that **inhabits Shigaraki**, which is why the two of them occupy S+ together rather than separately. Even reduced to a withered old body, he remains capable of restructuring other people's Quirks from a distance.

### 3. Izuku Midoriya — *One For All, fully realised*

One For All at 100% plus all six inherited Quirks: **Gearshift** (alters the speed and trajectory of anything he touches, including himself), **Fa Jin** (stored kinetic release), **Blackwhip**, **Danger Sense**, **Smokescreen** and **Float**.

Gearshift is the deciding factor. It lets Midoriya accelerate mid-air with no surface to push from and change the trajectory of his own body after a strike has begun, which breaks the physics every other character in the series is bound by. Combined with Danger Sense and One For All's raw output, he is the only hero who can fight Shigaraki and All For One on even terms.

He ranks third rather than first for one reason: **his body is not built for the power.** Every use damages him, and his sustained output is shorter than either antagonist's. ⚠️ By the epilogue he has lost One For All entirely.

### 4. All Might (prime)

Full One For All with no inherited Quirks — but the largest stockpile the power had ever accumulated up to that point, in the only body ever conditioned to carry it. Prime All Might defeated All For One at his strongest, **punched a weather system apart** with United States of Smash, and suppressed violent crime across an entire country for years through presence alone.

He is the benchmark the rest of the series is measured against. No character other than peak Midoriya and awakened Shigaraki reaches his level.

## S — Nation-Level

*Among the strongest beings in the world. Requires multiple top-tier combatants to defeat.*

### 5. Gigantomachia

Engineered by Doctor Garaki from multiple Quirks, with effectively limitless stamina. He crossed Japan on foot during the Paranormal Liberation War destroying everything in his path, and he is **never defeated by force** — only exhausted by Endeavor and put to sleep by a sedative that Class 1-A and 1-B had forced him to swallow eleven chapters earlier. Midnight's Somnambulist does not work on him.

He sits below S+ because he is not strategic, and because he can be hurt.

### 6. Endeavor — *Hellflame*

The strongest conventional fighter in the hero ranks after All Might's retirement. ⚠️ He defeats the first High-End Nomu, **Hood**, with a *PLUS ULTRA Prominence Burn* — but **not alone**: Hawks' feathers supply the speed and lift for the killing blow, and several other Pro Heroes are on the field. Endeavor survives a Nomu with Super Regeneration by burning it from the inside out, and only wins by realising he has to destroy the head.

He is held out of S+ by his own durability. Prominence Burn cooks him; he cannot sustain peak output, and in the war that follows he is broken by both Dabi and Shigaraki.

### 7. Dabi / Toya Todoroki — *Blueflame*

Flames hotter than Endeavor's, blue-white, capable of incinerating a city district. His final technique is effectively a suicide attack that would have killed everyone in range including himself.

He ranks below Endeavor for a single reason: **his body cannot tolerate his own Quirk.** He inherited his father's fire and his mother's *cold resistance* rather than her ice, so every use burns him alive. He is a weapon with a short fuse and no scabbard. ⚠️ He survives the final war by weeks and dies on life support.

### 8. Overhaul (fused form) — *Overhaul*

Disassembly and reassembly on contact, extended in his fused form to the entire Shie Hassaikai compound — he merges with the building and with Mimic, and rebuilds himself from any injury. He nearly kills Midoriya operating at a full 100%, and it takes Eri's Rewind pushing One For All to **Infinite 100%** — a *United States of World Smash* — to stop him.

His ceiling is genuine S-tier. His placement is limited by his psychology, which is unstable enough for Midoriya to exploit.

## B — Strong

*Competent licensed heroes, well-developed students, and dangerous specialist villains.*

**U.A. faculty.** Shota Aizawa · Hizashi Yamada (Present Mic) · Vlad King · Cementoss · Snipe · Ectoplasm · Hound Dog

> **Special note on Aizawa.** Erasure is S-tier *in effect* and Aizawa is B-tier in body. He removes the variable that makes an opponent dangerous, so he routinely survives and wins against characters far above him — but only while he can see them, and only until dry eye forces a blink. ⚠️ He loses an eye and a leg in the Paranormal Liberation War and still returns to fight. He is the clearest example of why these lists measure direct confrontational power rather than who wins.

**Pro Heroes.** Gran Torino · Crust · Yoroi Musha · Kamui Woods · Wash · Fat Gum · Rock Lock · Sir Nighteye

> **Sir Nighteye.** Foresight gives him one second of unavoidable precognition, which combined with his skill lets a physically ordinary man stand in the same fight as All For One and later Overhaul. Ranked B for what the Quirk makes possible rather than what his body can do.

**Students.** Tamaki Amajiki · Tenya Iida · Eijiro Kirishima · Mezo Shoji · Inasa Yoarashi · Tetsutetsu Tetsutetsu · Juzo Honenuki · Manga Fukidashi · Hiryu Rin · Togaru Kamakiri · Jurota Shishida · Hitoshi Shinso

> **Tamaki Amajiki** is borderline A. Manifest lets him grow anything he has eaten, and he defeats three of the Eight Bullets alone in a single corridor. He sits at the top of B only because his confidence collapses under pressure.
>
> **Mezo Shoji** earns his place late. ⚠️ In the final war he holds a line alone against a fifteen-thousand-strong heteromorph army and beats a transformed Spinner.
>
> **Inasa Yoarashi** (Shiketsu High, hero name *Gale Force*) controls wind with enough precision to move debris and people simultaneously without letting them collide, and can fly. He is a first-year who nearly matches Todoroki, and is ranked here rather than in A only because he is untrained.
>
> **Hitoshi Shinso** has the lowest raw power of anyone in B and belongs here for the same reason Aizawa does: Brainwashing ends fights regardless of the opponent's tier, and the Persona Cords let him beat people who would never answer him.

**Villains.** Mr. Compress · Moonfish · Mustard · Chronostasis · Mimic · Kendo Rappa · Hekiji Tengai · Slidin' Go · Trumpet · Spinner

> **Chronostasis** is ranked above his appearance: his clock-hand hair slows a target's personal time, and ⚠️ the wound he inflicts on Sir Nighteye is what kills him.
>
> **Mimic** merges into the Shie Hassaikai compound and controls an entire labyrinth — an area-effect threat with no personal defence.
>
> **Trumpet's** Incite amplifies the physical output of everyone who follows him, which across an army of one hundred thousand is a B-tier individual producing an S-tier force.
>
> ⚠️ **Spinner** is the most interesting case on this list. All For One gives him **Body Bulk** and **Scalemail**, turning him into a giant armoured berserker who briefly routes Mezo Shoji. In raw output that form is A-tier. He is ranked B because the Quirks destroy his reasoning — he cannot understand speech, fights on instinct, and becomes functionally a Nomu. Under the *consistency* criterion, borrowed power you cannot control is not your peak.

## C — Capable

*Solid fighters whose Quirks have real combat application but limited ceilings.*

**Class 1-A.** Rikido Sato · Mashirao Ojiro · Hanta Sero · Denki Kaminari · Koji Koda · Yuga Aoyama · Minoru Mineta

> **Mineta** belongs in C, not D. Pop Off is a genuine combat Quirk — the balls are adhesive, can be stacked into traps and walls, and he uses them for mobility. He wins through cunning rather than power, which is exactly what C measures.
>
> **Kaminari** is the clearest example of a Quirk with an S-tier ceiling and a D-tier user: exceeding his wattage limit turns him into a vacant, harmless thumbs-up. He is ranked on what he can reliably do.

**Class 1-B.** Neito Monoma · Nirengeki Shoda · Sen Kaibara · Kosei Tsuburaba · Shihai Kuroiro · Kojiro Bondo · Yosetsu Awase

> **Monoma** is permanently capped by his own Quirk. Copy replicates a Quirk but **not the accumulated power behind it** — when he copies One For All he gets the mechanism and none of the stockpile. He can hold four Quirks for five minutes. He is one of the most tactically valuable students at U.A. and one of the least individually powerful.

**Pro Heroes and sidekicks.** Power Loader · Manual · Death Arms · Backdraft · Selkie

**Villains.** Gentle Criminal · Skeptic · Doctor Garaki · and six of the Eight Bullets: Shin Nemoto (Confession) · Rikiya Katsukame (Energy Suck) · Toya Setsuno (Larceny) · Yu Hojo (Crystallize) · Soramitsu Tabe (Food) · Deidoro Sakaki (Sloshed)

> **Doctor Garaki** is ranked for what he personally brings to a fight, which is very little — he is an old man with a handful of minor Quirks. What he *built* is ranked separately: every Nomu in the series, including Gigantomachia and Hood, is his work. On preparation alone he would be S+; on personal combat power he is C.
>
> ⚠️ **Gentle Criminal** is pardoned after helping the heroes in the final war. He and **La Brava** marry and found a software company.

**Other schools.** Yo Shindo (Ketsubutsu Academy, *Vibrate* — destabilises terrain through touch)

## Observations

**Four characters sit at S+ and two of them are villains.** The series' power structure is deliberately top-heavy in villainy, and that is the narrative's point: hero society produced exactly one symbol strong enough to hold a country together, and the moment he retired, the replacements were outnumbered.

**No Class 1-A student other than Midoriya, Bakugo and Todoroki reaches A.** The gap between the three protagonists and the rest of their class is enormous, and Horikoshi never pretends otherwise — the Joint Training Battle exists partly to show that the rest of them are roughly as strong as their rivals.

**Three of the top eight are related to Endeavor.** Dabi is his son; Shoto is his son; Endeavor is himself. The strongest fire users in the series are one dysfunctional household, and two of the three are trying to kill the third.

**Support and control Quirks are systematically undervalued by this format.** Aizawa, Compress, Shinso, Kurogiri and Twice all defeat or neutralise opponents far above their tier. That is the strongest argument against any tier list — including this one — and it is worth saying out loud in a school project, because it is the single most interesting thing about how Horikoshi writes combat.

**The highest-ranked character who is not a combatant is Doctor Garaki,** and he is in C. Everything that makes him terrifying is in the C-tier body of an old man and the S-tier things standing behind him.
