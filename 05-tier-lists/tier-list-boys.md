## S+ — Existential Threat

Characters capable of ending a city, a country, or the existing social order on their own. Cannot be countered by conventional means.

---

### 1. Tomura Shigaraki — *Decay / All For One*

**Justification.** At his peak Shigaraki is the single most dangerous being in the series. His awakened **Decay** spreads through anything it touches without limit — removing contact no longer stops it — and it propagates through the ground, buildings, and air itself. Layered on top is **All For One**, granting him centuries of stolen Quirks, near-total regeneration, and a body modified by Doctor Garaki to survive its own power.

He is fast enough to evade every top-ranked hero simultaneously, durable enough to survive Endeavor's Prominence Burn and Mirko's assaults, and his offensive output is unblockable: anything that touches him is destroyed.

The only characters who have ever seriously threatened him are Midoriya and Star and Stripe, and Star and Stripe dies doing it.

---

### 2. All For One

**Justification.** Over a century of accumulated Quirks, near-immortality through stolen life-extension abilities, and the power to take or impose any Quirk on anyone. He has fought and injured every generation of One For All's wielders and lost only twice — to All Might at his prime, and to a fully realised Midoriya.

He ranks below Shigaraki only because Shigaraki's awakened Decay is a more absolute offensive tool than anything All For One possessed in his own body. His true peak, however, is the version that *inhabits* Shigaraki — which is why both occupy S+ together.

---

### 3. Izuku Midoriya — *One For All, fully realised*

**Justification.** One For All at 100% plus all six inherited Quirks: **Gearshift** (altering the speed and trajectory of anything he touches, including himself), **Fa Jin** (stored kinetic release), **Blackwhip** (binding tendrils), **Danger Sense** (hostile-intent detection), **Smokescreen**, and **Float**.

Gearshift is the deciding factor. It lets Midoriya move at speeds that break the conventional physics of the series, accelerating mid-air without a surface to push from. Combined with Danger Sense and One For All's raw output, he is the only hero capable of fighting Shigaraki and All For One on even terms.

He ranks third rather than first only because his body is not built for the power — every use damages him, and his sustained output is shorter than either antagonist's.

---

### 4. All Might (prime)

**Justification.** Full One For All with no inherited Quirks, but unmatched raw physical output. Prime All Might defeated All For One at his strongest, punched a weather system apart, and single-handedly suppressed violent crime across Japan for years through presence alone.

He is the benchmark against which every other character in the series is measured, and no character other than peak Midoriya and awakened Shigaraki reaches his level.

---

## S — Nation-Level

Among the strongest beings in the world. Requires multiple top-tier combatants to defeat.

---

### 5. Gigantomachia

**Justification.** A colossal creature engineered by Doctor Garaki from multiple Quirks, with near-limitless stamina and durability. He crossed Japan on foot during the Paranormal Liberation War, destroying everything in his path, and was **never defeated by force** — only restrained and put back to sleep.

He ranks below the S+ group because he is not intelligent enough to be strategic, and because Midoriya and All Might both demonstrated they could hurt him.

---

### 6. Endeavor — *Hellflame*

**Justification.** At his peak Endeavor solo-defeated the High-End Nomu **Hood** with **Prominence Burn**, an attack hot enough to vaporise a regenerating monster that had already shrugged off multiple Pro Heroes. He is the strongest conventional fire user in the series and, after All Might's retirement, the most powerful active hero.

He is held back from S+ by his durability: Prominence Burn burns his own body, and he cannot sustain peak output.

---

### 7. Dabi / Toya Todoroki — *Blueflame*

**Justification.** Flames hotter than Endeavor's, blue-white and capable of incinerating a city district. His final technique is effectively a suicide attack that would have killed everyone in range, including himself.

He ranks below Endeavor because his body cannot tolerate his own Quirk — he inherited his mother's cold resistance and his father's fire, so every use burns him. He is a weapon with a short fuse.

---

### 8. Overhaul (fused form) — *Overhaul*

**Justification.** Disassembly on contact, with the ability to reassemble himself from any injury — effectively immortality as long as he has matter to work with. In his fused form during the Shie Hassaikai raid he nearly killed Midoriya operating at a full 100%, and it took a **1,000,000% Delaware Detroit Smash** plus Eri's Rewind to stop him.

His ceiling is genuine S-tier; his ranking is limited by his psychology, which is unstable enough to be exploited.

## B — Strong

Competent licensed heroes, well-developed students, and dangerous specialist villains.

**Pro Heroes:** Doctor Garaki (with preparation) · Gran Torino · Crust · Yoroi Musha · Kamui Woods · Fat Gum · Selkie · Rock Lock · Manual · Death Arms · Backdraft

**U.A. Faculty:** Shota Aizawa (Erasure is S-tier in effect but he is physically limited) · Vlad King · Present Mic · Cementoss · Snipe · Ectoplasm · Hound Dog

**Students:** Mirio-adjacent third-years aside — Tamaki Amajiki · Tenya Iida · Eijiro Kirishima · Tetsutetsu Tetsutetsu · Juzo Honenuki · Manga Fukidashi · Hiryu Rin · Togaru Kamakiri · Jurota Shishida · Hitoshi Shinso

**Villains:** Mr. Compress · Moonfish · Trumpet · Skeptic · Slidin' Go · Kendo Rappa · Hekiji Tengai · Mimic

> **Special note on Shota Aizawa.** Aizawa's tier is a poor predictor of his effectiveness. **Erasure** removes the variable that makes an opponent dangerous, which means he routinely defeats characters several tiers above him — but only while he can see them, and only until his dry eye forces a blink. He is the clearest example of why these lists measure *direct confrontational power* rather than *who wins*.

## Observations

**Four characters sit at S+ and two of them are villains.** The series' power structure is deliberately top-heavy in villainy — which is the narrative's point. Hero society produced one symbol strong enough to hold the country together, and the moment he retired, the villains outnumbered the replacements.

**No Class 1-A student other than Midoriya, Bakugo, and Todoroki reaches A.** The gap between the three protagonists and the rest of their class is enormous, and Horikoshi never pretends otherwise.

**Support and control Quirks are systematically undervalued by this format.** Aizawa, Compress, Shinso, and Kurogiri all defeat opponents far above their tier. That is the strongest argument against any tier list — including this one.
