# Class 1-A

*The twenty first-year students of U.A. High School's Hero Course, homeroom class of Shota Aizawa. The protagonists of the series.*

## Main Characters

### Izuku Midoriya (緑谷 出久) — *Deku*

**Quirk:** One For All · **Debut:** Chapter 1 / Episode 1

The protagonist. Born Quirkless into a world where powers are the norm, Midoriya spent his childhood being told his dream was impossible — most cruelly by Katsuki Bakugo, who once advised him to jump off a roof and hope for a Quirk in his next life. Rather than become bitter, he became an obsessive student of heroism, filling thirteen notebooks with detailed analysis of every Pro Hero's abilities and tactics.

All Might chooses him not for talent but for one specific act: charging a sludge villain to save Bakugo while every licensed professional stood by. Midoriya inherits **One For All**, a power stockpiled across nine generations of wielders.

**Abilities.** One For All grants enormous superhuman strength, speed, and durability. Midoriya's body cannot initially withstand it — he breaks his bones on nearly every early use — so he develops **Full Cowling**, a technique that spreads the power evenly at a low percentage rather than concentrating it in one limb. Later he accesses **Shoot Style**, focusing power in his legs to spare his arms, and eventually inherits the Quirks of previous One For All users: **Blackwhip** (5th user, Daigoro Banjo), **Danger Sense** (4th user, Hikage Shinomori), **Smokescreen** (6th user, En), **Float** (7th user, Nana Shimura), **Fa Jin** (3rd user), and **Gearshift** (2nd user).

**Character.** Midoriya mutters constantly when analysing situations, cries easily, and apologises excessively. His defining trait is that he **moves before he thinks** when someone is in danger — the quality All Might required. The series' central critique of him is that this instinct is pathological: he will destroy himself to save one person, and in later arcs his attempt to protect everyone by working alone is treated as a serious mistake rather than a virtue.

**Trivia.** His hero name "Deku" was originally Bakugo's insult (a homophone for 木偶, "wooden doll" / "useless"). He adopts it after Uraraka tells him it sounds like *"I can do it."*

---

### Katsuki Bakugo (爆豪 勝己) — *Dynamight*

**Quirk:** Explosion · **Debut:** Chapter 1 / Episode 1

Midoriya's childhood friend and tormentor. Bakugo's Quirk lets him secrete nitroglycerin-like sweat from his palms and detonate it, producing concussive blasts powerful enough to propel him through the air and level buildings.

For most of the series Bakugo is the closest thing the protagonist has to an antagonist. He is arrogant, foul-tempered, and openly cruel, driven by a single goal: to surpass All Might and become the undisputed No. 1 Hero. His hatred of Midoriya stems from a specific psychological wound — a powerless boy kept trying to help him, which Bakugo experienced as being looked down on.

**Character arc.** Bakugo's development is the slowest and hardest-won in the series. He is kidnapped by the League of Villains and refuses to turn, which nearly costs him his life. He learns the truth about One For All and initially refuses to accept it. After All Might's retirement he blames himself, and in a pivotal later confrontation he apologises to Midoriya for years of abuse — a moment the series treats as genuine but insufficient. By the end he has become Midoriya's most reliable ally and the person who most consistently refuses to let him sacrifice himself.

**Trivia.** His hero name, "Great Explosion Murder God Dynamight," was chosen during a class exercise in which he rejected every suggestion and improvised something so aggressive that the class could not tell whether he was joking.

---

### Shoto Todoroki (轟 焦凍) — *Shoto*

**Quirk:** Half-Cold Half-Hot · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

The son of Endeavor, the No. 2 Hero. Todoroki's Quirk gives him ice on his right side and fire on his left — a combination deliberately engineered by his father, who married a woman with an ice Quirk specifically to produce a child who could surpass All Might.

The cost was his family. Endeavor's abuse drove Todoroki's mother, Rei, to a breakdown; she poured boiling water on the left side of his face, leaving the burn scar he still carries. For most of the series Todoroki refuses to use his fire half entirely, viewing it as his father's property rather than his own.

**Character arc.** The Sports Festival is his turning point. Midoriya, already broken from earlier matches, shouts *"It's your power, isn't it?!"* — and Todoroki uses his flames for the first time. What follows is not a reconciliation but a long, uncomfortable process: he confronts his mother, speaks to his sister Fuyumi, refuses to forgive his brother Natsuo's absence, and eventually faces **Dabi**, revealed to be his eldest brother Toya, presumed dead.

**Trivia.** His given name 焦凍 combines 焦 ("scorch") and 凍 ("freeze") — his Quirk written into his own name.

## Secondary Characters

### Ochaco Uraraka (麗日 お茶子) — *Uravity*

**Quirk:** Zero Gravity · **Debut:** Chapter 3 / Episode 3

Able to nullify gravity in anything she touches with the pads of her fingertips, releasing the effect by pressing her fingers together. Overuse causes severe nausea.

Uraraka is warm, direct, and unusually pragmatic — she became a hero explicitly to earn money for her parents' struggling construction business, which she states without embarrassment. She is Midoriya's first friend at U.A. and the character who names him "Deku" as something positive.

Her development centres on two things: her growing, largely unspoken affection for Midoriya, and her mentorship under **Gunhead**, whose combat style turns her from a support fighter into a competent close-quarters combatant. In later arcs she becomes the character who most directly addresses **Himiko Toga**, arguing that a girl who was told her feelings were monstrous should not have to be destroyed for them.

---

### Tenya Iida (飯田 天哉) — *Ingenium*

**Quirk:** Engine · **Debut:** Chapter 3 / Episode 3

Engine-like exhaust ports in his calves grant him extraordinary speed. He is Class 1-A's representative — earnest, rule-bound, and given to emphatic hand gestures.

Iida comes from a hero family; his older brother **Tensei** was the Pro Hero Ingenium until the Hero Killer Stain crippled him permanently. Iida's arc in Season 2 is a study in grief: he abandons his internship to hunt Stain alone, and is nearly killed for it. Midoriya and Todoroki save him, and he learns that his brother's understanding of heroism — protecting others rather than pursuing personal justice — was the correct one.

He later inherits the Ingenium name, though the series is careful to show that he carries it with ambivalence rather than pride.

---

### Eijiro Kirishima (切島 鋭児郎) — *Red Riot*

**Quirk:** Hardening · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Hardens his skin into a rock-like substance, granting high defence at the cost of mobility. With training he develops **Unbreakable**, a far more durable form.

Kirishima is loud, friendly, and obsessed with the idea of **manliness** — which he defines specifically as the willingness to stand in front of others. His backstory, revealed in Season 4, is that he was once timid and self-loathing, and reinvented himself around the Pro Hero **Crimson Riot**, whose philosophy was to face danger head-on with a grin. Kirishima's hardening is, in a sense, a personality he built deliberately.

He is Bakugo's closest friend and the person who most consistently reaches him emotionally. He is also the student who goes back for Bakugo during the Forest Training Camp rescue.

---

### Momo Yaoyorozu (八百万 百) — *Creati*

**Quirk:** Creation · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Creates any non-living object she can visualise the molecular structure of, drawing on her body's lipid reserves. The richest student in Class 1-A and its vice-representative.

Yaoyorozu is exceptionally intelligent — arguably the smartest tactician in the class — but suffers acute self-doubt. Her arc in the Final Exams, where she must overcome freezing under Aizawa's Erasure alongside Todoroki, is the beginning of her learning to trust her own judgement. By the Paranormal Liberation War she is leading groups of Pro Heroes.

---

### Tsuyu Asui (蛙吹 梅雨) — *Froppy*

**Quirk:** Frog · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Full frog physiology: adhesive pads, a prehensile tongue, enhanced jumping, camouflage, and a stomach that can eject its contents. She is also highly sensitive to cold.

Asui is blunt to the point of bluntness-as-kindness. She says exactly what she thinks, calls people by their given names with a *-chan* suffix, and is the emotional anchor of Class 1-A's female students. Her most important moment comes in Season 2, when she tells Midoriya plainly that she has noticed he is lying about his Quirk — and that she will not push, because being his friend matters more than knowing.

---

### Fumikage Tokoyami (常闇 踏陰) — *Tsukuyomi*

**Quirk:** Dark Shadow · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Hosts a sentient shadow entity named **Dark Shadow**, which becomes vastly more powerful — and less controllable — in darkness.

Tokoyami is theatrical, speaks in an archaic register, and is deeply devoted to his classmates. His Quirk is one of the most dangerous in Class 1-A precisely because it has a will of its own: during the Forest Training Camp, Dark Shadow goes berserk in the dark and Tokoyami nearly kills his own friends. He later masters it, and during the Paranormal Liberation War Dark Shadow manifests at enormous scale to protect others.

---

### Kyoka Jiro (耳郎 響香) — *Earphone Jack*

**Quirk:** Earphone Jack · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Her earlobes are long, pluggable jacks that she can insert into surfaces to detect vibrations, or use to channel her own heartbeat as a concussive sonic attack.

Jiro is a musician — bass guitar and vocals — who was initially ashamed of it, believing music was not a serious pursuit for a hero. Her arc concludes in the School Festival, where she leads Class 1-A's performance and accepts that what she loves is not a distraction from heroism but part of it.

---

### Denki Kaminari (上鳴 電気) — *Chargebolt*

**Quirk:** Electrification · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Generates and discharges electricity. His critical weakness: exceeding his wattage limit fries his own brain, leaving him in a vacant, thumbs-up-making state he calls "**wheeey**."

Kaminari is the class clown and self-professed ladies' man. He develops a directional discharge weapon (a sharpshooter-style targeting device) to overcome his Quirk's imprecision, and grows considerably more competent across the series — though he remains one of the weaker fighters in the class.

---

### Mina Ashido (芦戸 三奈) — *Pinky*

**Quirk:** Acid · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Secretes corrosive liquid from her skin with variable potency and viscosity; her body is immune to it. She has pink skin, black-and-yellow eyes, and small horns — a mutation-type presentation.

Ashido is energetic, athletic, and the class's best dancer. Notably, she **failed her final exams** and has among the lowest academic grades in Class 1-A, yet she is one of the most emotionally perceptive students — she is the first to notice that something is wrong with Todoroki, and she choreographs the School Festival performance.

---

### Hanta Sero (瀬呂 範太) — *Cellophane*

**Quirk:** Tape · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Dispenses strong, elastic cellophane-like tape from dispensers in his elbows. Excellent for mobility, restraint, and rescue.

Sero is easygoing and often functions as the class's informal mediator — he is usually the one who defuses tension between Bakugo and everyone else. Tactically he is one of the most useful support fighters in Class 1-A, though he rarely takes centre stage.

---

### Mashirao Ojiro (尾白 猿夫) — *Tailman*

**Quirk:** Tail · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

A strong, prehensile tail used for combat and balance; combined with genuine martial arts training.

Ojiro is one of the most competent hand-to-hand fighters in Class 1-A but has one of the least flashy Quirks, which the series explicitly treats as a source of insecurity for him. He withdrew from the Sports Festival tournament on principle, believing he had only advanced through another student's Quirk rather than his own skill — a decision the narrative frames as honourable and self-defeating simultaneously.

---

### Yuga Aoyama (青山 優雅) — *Can't Stop Twinkling*

**Quirk:** Navel Laser · **Debut:** Chapter 4 / Episode 4

Fires a sparkling laser from his navel. Overuse causes severe stomach pain.

Aoyama is flamboyant, speaks in a mixture of Japanese and French, and obsessively centres himself in conversation. Beneath the performance is a deeply lonely boy who was **born Quirkless** — like Midoriya — and whose parents made a bargain with **All For One** to give him a power. The Navel Laser damages his body every time he uses it. This secret, and its eventual exposure, drives one of the series' most significant late revelations.

---

### Toru Hagakure (葉隠 透) — *Invisible Girl*

**Quirk:** Invisibility · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Permanently invisible. She can refract light through her body to produce blinding flashes, and later learns to bend light offensively.

Because her appearance is unknown for most of the series, Hagakure functions largely as comic relief and as a tactical wildcard — she is undetectable, which makes her ideal for stealth and reconnaissance. She is cheerful, direct, and unbothered by the attention her condition draws.

---

### Mezo Shoji (障子 目蔵) — *Tentacole*

**Quirk:** Dupli-Arms · **Debut:** Chapter 4 / Episode 4

Can reproduce any of his organs at the ends of multiple replicating arms — eyes, ears, mouths, feet — giving him exceptional sensory range and combat versatility.

Shoji wears a mask over the lower half of his face and is quiet and observant. He was bullied as a child for his appearance, and his arc centres on learning that his body is not something to hide. He is intensely protective of Tokoyami and serves as the class's best reconnaissance asset.

---

### Rikido Sato (砂藤 力道) — *Sugarman*

**Quirk:** Sugar Rush · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Gains a fivefold strength boost for a limited period after consuming sugar, at the cost of progressively impaired cognition the longer it lasts.

Sato is a large, gentle student and an excellent baker — he frequently makes cakes and sweets for the class. He is straightforward and dependable rather than dramatic, and the series uses him as an example of an ordinary, competent hero-in-training.

---

### Koji Koda (口田 甲司) — *Anima*

**Quirk:** Anivoice · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Commands animals through speech. Later develops the ability to affect insects and to accelerate biological processes.

Koda is painfully shy and barely speaks, which creates an obvious tension with a Quirk that requires talking. His development across the series is largely about overcoming that fear. He has a strong aversion to insects, which he must confront during the Provisional Licence Exam.

---

### Minoru Mineta (峰田 実) — *Grape Juice*

**Quirk:** Pop Off · **Debut:** Chapter 5 / Episode 5

Plucks adhesive, bounce-capable spheres from his scalp and throws them; they stick to almost anything and can support considerable weight.

Mineta is short, cowardly, and relentlessly lecherous — the series' most frequently criticised character, and deliberately so. His comedic function is undercut by moments of genuine tactical cleverness: he survives the U.S.J. attack through ingenuity rather than strength, and he passes the Provisional Licence Exam by out-thinking Midnight. Horikoshi uses him to explore what happens when a person with no heroic qualities ends up in a heroic environment anyway.
