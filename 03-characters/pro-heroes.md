## Enji Todoroki (轟 炎司) — *Endeavor*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Hellflame |
| **Rank** | No. 1 (from Season 4) |
| **Voice (JP)** | Tetsu Inada |
| **Voice (EN)** | Patrick Seitz |
| **Debut** | Season 2 |

The most complex adult character in the series. Endeavor generates and controls intense flames from his entire body, with his signature technique **Prominence Burn** — a focused blast hot enough to destroy a High-End Nomu.

**The sin.** Endeavor spent his life obsessed with surpassing All Might. When he could not, he decided to **breed** someone who could. He arranged a marriage with **Rei**, a woman with an ice Quirk, specifically to produce a child with both fire and ice. He got **Shoto**.

He then trained Shoto relentlessly from age five, ignoring his other three children — **Toya**, **Fuyumi**, and **Natsuo** — and abusing Rei to the point of a mental breakdown. She poured boiling water on Shoto's face. Endeavor had her committed.

Toya, who had inherited his father's fire but his mother's cold intolerance, burned himself trying to earn Endeavor's approval. Endeavor declared him too weak and stopped training him. **Toya died** — or was believed to. He survived, and became **Dabi**.

**The redemption arc.** After All Might retires, Endeavor becomes No. 1 by default — a rank he did not earn and does not want. Rather than celebrate, he declares publicly that he will make amends and asks the country to judge him by what he does next.

In Season 5 he fights the High-End Nomu **Hood** alone in Jaku City, is nearly killed, and wins with Prominence Burn. He turns to the civilians and says: *"It's okay now. I am here to save you"* — deliberately borrowing All Might's phrase, acknowledging that he is performing a symbol he never earned. Dabi attacks him immediately afterward and reveals who he is.

**Why he matters.** Endeavor is the series' argument that redemption is not the same as forgiveness. He is never forgiven. Rei will not look at him; Natsuo refuses to speak to him; Shoto tolerates him at best. What he gets instead is **obligation** — the requirement to keep doing the right thing for the rest of his life, without relief. Horikoshi never resolves it, and that is deliberate.

## Keigo Takami (鷹見 啓悟) — *Hawks*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Fierce Wings |
| **Rank** | No. 2 |
| **Voice (JP)** | Yuichi Nakamura |
| **Voice (EN)** | Zeno Robinson |
| **Debut** | Season 4 |

Red-feathered wings that he controls individually and telekinetically. Each feather can be directed separately for reconnaissance, attack, restraint, or rescue; he can regrow them, though it takes time. He is the fastest hero in Japan.

**Public persona.** Hawks is flippant, casual, and appears not to take anything seriously. He says he wants a world where heroes have too much free time. He reached the top ten at age 22, the youngest in history.

**The truth.** Hawks was recruited as a child by the **Hero Public Safety Commission**, which raised and trained him as an operative rather than a hero. From Season 5 he is working **undercover inside the Paranormal Liberation Front**, feeding intelligence to the Commission while befriending Dabi — who suspects him the entire time.

His undercover work requires him to do things a hero should not. He kills **Twice** — a villain he had genuinely come to like — because Twice's Double Quirk was too dangerous to leave alive and because Twice was about to warn the Front. The series does not soften this. Hawks is shown to be damaged by it.

**Character.** Hawks is the series' clearest critique of institutional heroism: a man raised to be a tool, who performs cheerfulness because no one ever taught him anything else. His line to Dabi — that he wants heroes to have free time — is sincere, and it is also the wish of someone who has never had any.

## Tsunagu Hakamada (袴田 綱) — *Best Jeanist*

**Quirk:** Fiber Master · **Voice (JP):** Hikaru Midorikawa · **Voice (EN):** Micah Solusod

Controls the fibres in any fabric, allowing him to bind, immobilise, and manipulate opponents through their clothing. He is precise, elegant, and obsessive about presentation — he personally corrected Bakugo's appearance and speech during Bakugo's internship.

Best Jeanist was critically injured by All For One during the Kamino Ward incident and spent the following period in a coma. He was believed dead by the public, which is how the No. 3 rank was left vacant on the chart. He later recovers and returns to active duty.

---

## Shinya Kamihara (紙原 忍夜) — *Edgeshot*

**Quirk:** Foldabody · **Voice (JP):** Kenta Kamakari · **Voice (EN):** John Burgmeier

Can fold his body flat and thin, to a width of half a millimetre, giving him extreme speed, the ability to slip into gaps, and a cutting edge. A ninja-themed hero and one of the most tactically intelligent Pro Heroes in the series. He takes Kirishima and Bakugo on during the Remedial Course period.

---

## Rumi Usagiyama (兎山  ルミ) — *Mirko*

**Quirk:** Rabbit · **Voice (JP):** Sayaka Kinoshita · **Voice (EN):** Anairis Quiñones

A rabbit mutation-type Quirk granting extraordinary leg strength, speed, and jumping ability. Mirko fights exclusively with her legs and has no ranged options whatsoever — a deliberate design choice that reflects her personality.

She is the most aggressive of the top-ranked heroes: she operates solo, refuses sidekicks, and charges into situations that other heroes avoid. During the Paranormal Liberation War she fights multiple High-End Nomu alone and loses an arm and part of her other limbs, continuing to fight throughout. She becomes one of the series' most popular characters precisely because she refuses to stop.

---

## Yu Takeyama (岳 山 優) — *Mt. Lady*

**Quirk:** Gigantification · **Voice (JP):** Kaori Nazuka · **Voice (EN):** Jamie Marchi

Grows to a fixed height of 20.6 metres. Mt. Lady is frank about her motivations: she entered heroism for fame and money, and says so on camera. Her arc across the series is a slow, unglamorous shift toward genuine heroism — she becomes increasingly willing to take risks that do not benefit her public image, and her partnership with **Kamui Woods** and **Edgeshot** develops into real teamwork.

She is the series' clearest example of Stain's critique being *correct* about some heroes, and then complicated by those heroes growing anyway.

## Shinji Nishiya (西宮 真二) — *Kamui Woods*

**Quirk:** Arbor · **Voice (JP):** Masamichi Kitada · **Voice (EN):** Aaron Roberts

Manipulates his body's wood-like composition, extending branches to restrain or strike. His signature technique, **Lacquer Chain Prison**, immobilises large groups instantly. He is a serious, professional hero and Mt. Lady's reluctant partner.

---

## Taishiro Toyomitsu (豊満 太志郎) — *Fat Gum*

**Quirk:** Fat Absorption · **Voice (JP):** Kazuyuki Okitsu · **Voice (EN):** Kyle Hebert

His body absorbs kinetic impact, storing it and releasing it as a single devastating counterattack. The more damage he takes, the more powerful he becomes — at the cost of burning through his body mass.

Fat Gum is cheerful, loud, and food-obsessed. He is also the mentor who takes **Eijiro Kirishima** and **Tamaki Amajiki** under his wing during the Shie Hassaikai raid, where his fight against **Kendo Rappa** is one of Season 4's best sequences. He is a genuinely excellent teacher: he recognises Kirishima's courage immediately and builds on it rather than correcting it.

---

## Ryuko Tatsuma (竜間 龍子) — *Ryukyu*

**Quirk:** Dragon · **Voice (JP):** Kaori Yagi · **Voice (EN):** Katelyn Barr

Transforms into a full dragon. Ryukyu is calm, professional, and runs one of the most respected agencies in Japan. She mentors **Ochaco Uraraka**, **Tsuyu Asui**, and **Nejire Hado** during their work-study, and is one of the most competent leaders in the Paranormal Liberation War.

---

## Mirai Sasaki (佐々木 未来) — *Sir Nighteye*

**Quirk:** Foresight · **Voice (JP):** Shin-ichiro Miki · **Voice (EN):** Brandon McInnis

All Might's former sidekick. Foresight lets him see exactly one hour of a person's future, once per day, and it **cannot be turned off** — everything he sees is guaranteed to happen.

Nighteye is humourless, exacting, and deeply devoted to All Might. Their friendship ended over a professional disagreement: Nighteye believed All Might should retire while he still could, and All Might refused. They did not speak for years.

He groomed **Mirio Togata** to inherit One For All, and considered Mirio objectively superior to Midoriya. When All Might chose Midoriya instead, Nighteye used Foresight on him and saw **Midoriya's death**. He spent the entire Shie Hassaikai arc trying to prevent it.

He is mortally wounded by Overhaul during the raid. As he dies, he reconciles with All Might — telling him he should have smiled more — and his last words are that he wants Midoriya to have a bright future. **Midoriya survives because of Eri's Rewind**, which Nighteye never learned about. Whether his vision was wrong, or whether it was fulfilled in a way he could not interpret, is left deliberately unresolved.

## Sorahiko Torino (鳥野 空彦) — *Gran Torino*

**Quirk:** Jet · **Voice (JP):** Kenichi Ogata · **Voice (EN):** Charles C. Campbell

All Might's teacher and Nana Shimura's mentor. He propels himself with jets from his feet at speeds that make him nearly untouchable. Small, elderly, and apparently senile, he is in fact the sharpest analyst in the series.

Gran Torino trains Midoriya after the Sports Festival and teaches him the single most important lesson about One For All: that Midoriya is treating it as a special move rather than as a part of his body. This insight produces **Full Cowling**.

He is also the character who most directly confronts All Might with his failures — including the fact that All Might never investigated Nana Shimura's family, which is how Tenko Shimura ended up with All For One.

---

## Kugo Sakamata (坂又 空吾) — *Gang Orca*

**Quirk:** Orca · **Voice (JP):** Shuhei Matsuda · **Voice (EN):** Tyler Walker

A killer-whale mutation hero who uses ultrasonic attacks. He is ranked highly and is feared by students and villains alike for his intimidating appearance — which he leans into deliberately. He supervises Bakugo and Todoroki's remedial licence course and delivers the season's clearest critique of both: Bakugo's contempt for others and Todoroki's emotional withdrawal are disqualifying flaws, not personality quirks.

## The Wild, Wild Pussycats

A four-person hero team specialising in mountain rescue, introduced in Season 3.

| Member | Real name | Quirk |
|---|---|---|
| **Mandalay** | Shino Magi | Telepath — sends thoughts directly into others' minds |
| **Pixie-Bob** | Ryu Shido | Earth Flow — manipulates soil |
| **Ragdoll** | Tomoko Shiretoko | Search — locates and monitors up to 100 people, knowing their exact position and condition |
| **Tiger** | Yu Takei | Pliocroft — softens and flexes his own muscles for enhanced strength |

During the Forest Training Camp attack, **Ragdoll's Search Quirk is stolen** by All For One's agents — the first on-screen confirmation that Quirks can be permanently taken. Ragdoll subsequently retires from hero work, traumatised.
