# Characters: Index

*A reference guide to the cast of My Hero Academia, organised by affiliation.*

---

## How to Use This Section

Characters are grouped into seven pages by their primary affiliation. Each profile follows a consistent format:

- **Name** with original kanji
- **Hero / Villain name**
- **Quirk** and how it works
- **Birthday**, height and age where the series states them
- **Voice actors** (Japanese and English)
- **First appearance**
- A written **profile** covering role, personality, and character development
- **Trivia** where relevant

Profiles are weighted toward characters who matter most to Seasons 1–5, but include later developments where they change how an earlier arc should be understood.

> ⚠️ **Spoiler note.** Several pages contain information from beyond Season 5, because the series' central reveals retroactively change what earlier events mean. Anything past Season 5 is flagged inline with ⚠️ or a **SPOILER** heading. The pages most affected are [Villains](league-of-villains.md), [Supporting Cast](supporting-cast.md), [Class 1-A](class-1-a.md) and [Pro Heroes](pro-heroes.md).

## Recurring Confusions Worth Settling Once

| Common mistake | Correct answer |
|---|---|
| "The Big Three debut in Season 4." | Season 3, episodes 62–63. |
| "Mei Hatsume debuts in Season 3." | Season 2, episode 15 — *"Roaring Sports Festival."* She becomes a recurring character in Season 3. |
| "Chronostasis and Mimic are Eight Bullets." | They are senior members of the Shie Hassaikai who work *above* the Bullets. The eight are Nemoto, Katsukame, Setsuno, Hojo, Tabe, Sakaki, Rappa and Tengai. |
| "Centipeder's real name is Sansa Tamakawa." | Juzo Moashi. Sansa Tamagawa is a different character. |
| "Dabi is revealed as Toya Todoroki in Season 5." | Season 6, episode 11 — *"Dabi's Dance,"* manga chapter 290. |
| "Mt. Lady is in the Season 4 top ten." | Rank 10 is Ryukyu. Mt. Lady is not on the chart. |
| "Midoriya beats Overhaul with 1,000,000%." | He beats **Muscular** at 1,000,000% (Season 3, episode 42). Overhaul falls to Eri's Rewind pushing One For All to **Infinite 100%** (Season 4). |
