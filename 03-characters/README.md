# Characters: Index

*A reference guide to the cast of My Hero Academia, organised by affiliation.*

---

## How to Use This Section

Characters are grouped into seven pages by their primary affiliation. Each profile follows a consistent format:

- **Name** with original kanji
- **Hero / Villain name**
- **Quirk** and how it works
- **Voice actors** (Japanese and English)
- **First appearance**
- A written **profile** covering role, personality, and character development
- **Trivia** where relevant

Profiles are weighted toward characters who matter most to Seasons 1–5, but include later developments where they change how an earlier arc should be understood.

> **Spoiler note:** The [Villains](league-of-villains.md) and [Supporting Cast](supporting-cast.md) pages contain significant spoilers for events beyond Season 5.
