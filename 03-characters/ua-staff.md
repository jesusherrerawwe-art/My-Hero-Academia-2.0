# U.A. High School Staff

*The faculty of Japan's most prestigious hero academy.*

---

## Toshinori Yagi (八木 俊典) — *All Might*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | One For All (former holder) |
| **Role** | Heroics teacher, Class 1-A (Seasons 1–3) |
| **Voice (JP)** | Kenta Miyake |
| **Voice (EN)** | Christopher Sabat |
| **Debut** | Chapter 1 / Episode 1 |

The **Symbol of Peace** and, for most of the series, the No. 1 Hero in Japan. All Might is the ninth wielder of **One For All**, inheriting it from his mentor **Nana Shimura**. He held the top rank for years and single-handedly suppressed violent crime across the country — not through policing, but through the psychological effect of his presence.

**The secret.** All Might was grievously wounded in his first battle with **All For One**, losing most of his stomach and one lung. Since then he has been able to maintain his muscular hero form for only a few hours per day — later, only minutes. In his true form he is **Toshinori Yagi**: a skeletal, gaunt man who coughs blood and looks nothing like the hero on every billboard in Japan.

This is the structural engine of the entire series. All Might is performing invulnerability. Every smile is an act of maintenance — he smiles so that people will not be afraid.

**Character.** He is theatrical, boisterous, and completely sincere. He also makes a series of serious mistakes: he chooses Midoriya impulsively, he conceals his condition from the people around him, and his single-minded pursuit of All For One leaves his successor's family unaddressed — a failure that directly produces Tomura Shigaraki.

**Kamino Ward (Season 3).** His final battle. Already operating past his time limit, he fights All For One on live television, wins with a single **United States of Smash**, and collapses into his true form in front of the entire country. He points at the camera and says, *"Next... it's your turn."* Then he retires.

**After retirement.** He becomes a teacher at U.A. and Midoriya's full-time mentor, working to help him master One For All's inherited Quirks. He is no longer a symbol; he is a man trying to prepare someone else. His relationship with Midoriya in the later arcs — in which he must accept that Midoriya will surpass him and may die doing it — is the emotional core of the series' second half.

**Trivia.** His name, 八木 俊典, is unremarkable by design. "All Might" was chosen by Nana Shimura, who wanted something that sounded like an American comic book.

## Shota Aizawa (相澤 消太) — *Eraser Head*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Erasure |
| **Role** | Class 1-A homeroom teacher |
| **Voice (JP)** | Junichi Suwabe |
| **Voice (EN)** | Alex Organ (S1); Christopher Wehkamp (S2 onward) |
| **Debut** | Chapter 3 / Episode 4 |

Aizawa's Quirk nullifies any other Quirk he can see, though it does not work on mutation-type Quirks and causes him severe dry eye. He fights using a **capture weapon** — a carbon-fibre scarf — combined with hand-to-hand technique, making him effective precisely because he removes his opponents' advantage rather than overpowering them.

**Character.** Aizawa is exhausted, blunt, and openly contemptuous of hero theatrics. He sleeps in a yellow sleeping bag between classes. He is also, by a considerable margin, the most protective teacher at U.A.

His defining philosophy is **rationality as care**. He expels students who lack potential — not out of cruelty, but because he believes sending an unprepared person into hero work is a death sentence. He tells Midoriya in Episode 4 that a hero who breaks his own body to save one person is useless if he dies doing it.

**Backstory.** Aizawa lost his close friend **Oboro Shirakumo** while they were students. Shirakumo's body was later used by Doctor Garaki to create **Kurogiri**, the League of Villains' warp-gate Nomu. Aizawa spends years not knowing this. When he learns it in Season 5, and later confronts the residual personality inside Kurogiri, it becomes one of the most emotionally devastating threads in the series.

**Forest Training Camp (Season 3).** He fights Moonfish and the rest of the Vanguard Action Squad alone, through the forest, to keep them away from his students. He is beaten systematically — arms broken, body badly burned and cut — and is hospitalised for weeks. He recovers and returns to teaching.

> **A common misconception.** Aizawa does *not* lose his eye or his leg in Season 3. That happens considerably later, during the **Paranormal Liberation War** (Season 6): Shigaraki strikes him with a Quirk-erasing bullet, Aizawa amputates his own leg to stop the drug spreading, and Shigaraki claws out his right eye. He subsequently uses a prosthetic leg and an eyepatch, and his Erasure is left effectively unusable.

## Nezu (根津) — *Principal*

**Quirk:** High Spec · **Voice (JP):** Yasuhiro Takato · **Voice (EN):** Jerry Jewell

An animal — apparently a dog, mouse, or bear, he declines to specify — who was given human-level intelligence through experimentation before Quirks were widespread. High Spec grants him intellect far beyond any human.

Nezu is polite, tea-drinking, and quietly ruthless. He designed U.A.'s current structure, including the barrier system and the dorm programme. He is the character who most clearly understands that U.A. is a target, and who acts on that understanding before anyone else does.

He is also one of the few characters who treats the students as people rather than assets — a contrast to the Hero Public Safety Commission.

## Hizashi Yamada (山田 陽射) — *Present Mic*

**Quirk:** Voice · **Voice (JP):** Hiroyuki Yoshino · **Voice (EN):** Sonny Strait (eps. 1–16); Dave Trosko (eps. 17 onward)

Amplifies his voice into a directional sonic attack. He is U.A.'s English teacher and the announcer for every major event, speaking in an exaggerated radio-DJ register ("**YEEEEAH!**").

Beneath the performance, Yamada is Aizawa's oldest friend and the series' most emotionally articulate adult. He and Aizawa were classmates with Oboro Shirakumo, and Yamada carries that loss openly in a way Aizawa cannot. In Season 5 he is the one who breaks through to Kurogiri by calling him by Shirakumo's name.

## Nemuri Kayama (香山 眠) — *Midnight*

**Quirk:** Somnambulist · **Voice (JP):** Akeno Watanabe · **Voice (EN):** Elizabeth Maxwell

Emits a sleep-inducing aroma from her skin; men are affected more strongly than women. She is U.A.'s Modern Hero Art History teacher and the referee for the Sports Festival.

Midnight is confident, flirtatious, and theatrical, and she is unapologetic about the fact that her hero persona is built on her appearance. She is also a genuinely capable combatant and one of the faculty's most decisive members during the U.S.J. incident and the Paranormal Liberation War.

## Anan Kurose (黒瀬 穴南) — *Thirteen*

**Quirk:** Black Hole · **Voice (JP):** Inuko Inuyama · **Voice (EN):** Morgan Berry

The rescue specialist who designed and operates the **U.S.J.** facility. Thirteen's Quirk creates miniature black holes from their fingertips capable of disintegrating anything — a power they almost never use offensively, having chosen a rescue career instead.

Thirteen wears a spacesuit-style costume because their Quirk would destroy their own body if used carelessly. During the U.S.J. attack they attempt to trap Kurogiri; he opens a warp gate inside the attack and Thirteen's own Black Hole destroys their body. They survive only through Recovery Girl's treatment.

Thirteen's gender is never specified in the series.

## Chiyo Shuzenji (修復 千代) — *Recovery Girl*

**Quirk:** Heal · **Voice (JP):** Etsuko Kozakura · **Voice (EN):** Juli Erickson (S1); Luci Christian (S2 onward)

U.A.'s school nurse. Her Quirk accelerates the target's natural healing by transferring her own stamina — meaning the patient must have enough energy to heal, and Recovery Girl cannot cure fatal wounds.

She is elderly, short-tempered, and the only person at U.A. who regularly scolds All Might to his face. She kisses patients' foreheads to activate Heal, which the students find deeply embarrassing.

Her repeated warnings to Midoriya — that she cannot keep fixing him, that he will eventually run out of body to heal — are the series' most consistent moral voice on self-sacrifice.

## Sekijiro Kan (管 赤慈郎) — *Vlad King*

**Quirk:** Blood Control · **Voice (JP):** Shuhei Matsuda · **Voice (EN):** Ben Bryant

Manipulates his own blood outside his body, hardening it into constructs and blades. Class 1-B's homeroom teacher.

Vlad King is Aizawa's professional counterpart and occasional rival. He is considerably more conventional in his teaching style — supportive, straightforward, and proud of his class — and defends Class 1-B against Monoma's complaints while privately agreeing that they deserve more recognition.

---

## Ken Ishiyama (石山 堅) — *Cementoss*

**Quirk:** Cement · **Voice (JP):** Kenta Okuma · **Voice (EN):** Chris Rager

Controls any cement-based material. U.A.'s Modern Literature teacher and a highly effective area-control combatant; during the Final Exams he immobilises Kirishima and Ashido by manipulating the arena itself.

---

## Ectoplasm

**Quirk:** Clones · **Voice (JP):** Masakazu Nishida · **Voice (EN):** Mike McFarland

Produces ectoplasm from his mouth that he can transform into clones of himself, up to a limit of around thirty. U.A.'s mathematics teacher. His clones act independently and can coordinate, making him effective against multiple opponents simultaneously.

---

## Snipe

**Quirk:** Homing · **Voice (JP):** Toru Nara · **Voice (EN):** David Wald

A revolver-wielding hero whose bullets can be steered mid-flight. U.A.'s heroics teacher. He wears a Western-style mask and is one of the faculty's most reliable long-range specialists.

---

## Higari Maijima (真島 輝) — *Power Loader*

**Quirk:** Iron Claws · **Voice (JP):** Yo Kitazawa · **Voice (EN):** David Wilson-Brown

Head of U.A.'s **Support Course** and the teacher of the development studio where **Mei Hatsume** works. His Quirk lets him dig and cut through metal with claw-like fingertips. He is a patient mentor to support students and one of the most important non-combat faculty in the series.

---

## Ryo Inui (乾 彪) — *Hound Dog*

**Quirk:** Dog · **Voice (JP):** Eiji Hanawa · **Voice (EN):** Zach Bolton

U.A.'s guidance counsellor. He has a permanent canine mutation and an enhanced sense of smell roughly 8,000 times a human's. He is aggressive, direct, and unexpectedly good at his job — the students come to him with genuine problems, and he handles them competently.

---

## Lunch Rush

**Role:** Head chef · **Voice (JP):** Keisuke Ueda · **Voice (EN):** Tyler Walker

U.A.'s cafeteria chef. His Quirk lets him cook multiple dishes simultaneously at high speed. He appears mostly in comedy scenes but is beloved by the students, who consider his cooking one of the best things about living in the dorms.
