## Leading Characters

### Itsuka Kendo (拳藤 一佳) — *Battle Fist*

**Quirk:** Big Fist · **Debut:** Chapter 32 / Season 2

Expands her hands and forearms to enormous size, granting tremendous striking power and the ability to shield others.

Kendo is Class 1-B's representative and its de facto leader — the closest thing the series has to a female counterpart to Tenya Iida, though considerably more relaxed about rules. She is sensible, direct, and physically capable of restraining **Neito Monoma** whenever he starts a fight with Class 1-A, which is often. She karate-chops the back of his neck and drags him away; it is one of the series' most reliable running gags.

Kendo is one of the strongest hand-to-hand fighters among the first-years and takes her role as a class leader seriously. She interned at the **Battle Fist Agency** and later fights in the Paranormal Liberation War.

**Trivia.** She and Monoma have known each other since middle school, where she was already managing his behaviour.

---

### Neito Monoma (物間 寧人) — *Phantom Thief*

**Quirk:** Copy · **Debut:** Chapter 32 / Season 2

Copies any Quirk he touches, retaining it for a limited period. He can hold up to four or five at once. The critical limitation: **he copies the Quirk, not its accumulated resources or transformations.** A copied strength-enhancement Quirk gives him a baseline version, not the user's developed power.

Monoma is the series' most committed agent of chaos. His entire personality is organised around an obsessive rivalry with Class 1-A, whom he considers arrogant for receiving more attention. He taunts them relentlessly, laughs maniacally, and is immediately subdued by Kendo.

Beneath the performance, Monoma is perceptive and genuinely brave. His Copy Quirk makes him an outstanding support fighter and tactician — he can adapt mid-battle in ways no other student can. During the **Joint Training Arc**, he copies **One For All** from Midoriya and receives nothing, which produces the season's most important revelation: One For All is not a simple ability but a stored legacy that cannot be duplicated.

**Character.** Monoma's rivalry is partly a defence mechanism. He is acutely aware that his Quirk is considered weak on its own, and his need to prove Class 1-B's worth is sincere even when his methods are absurd. By the end of the series he has become one of Midoriya's most dependable allies — while still refusing to admit it.

---

### Tetsutetsu Tetsutetsu (鉄哲 徹鐵) — *Real Steel*

**Quirk:** Steel · **Debut:** Chapter 32 / Season 2

Turns his entire body into steel. Simple, direct, and extremely effective in close combat.

Tetsutetsu is loud, hot-blooded, and devoted to the idea of manly confrontation — which makes him the natural rival and eventual close friend of **Eijiro Kirishima**. The two are deliberately written as parallels: same philosophy, same temperament, nearly identical combat approach, different classes. They bond during the Forest Training Camp and the Joint Training Arc.

His Quirk's weakness is heat and prolonged strain — steel softens, and he has a limited duration before his body gives out.

---

### Ibara Shiozaki (塩崎 茨) — *Vine*

**Quirk:** Vines · **Debut:** Chapter 32 / Season 2

Her hair is composed of living vines she can extend, entangle, and use defensively. She can regrow them by consuming water and sunlight.

Shiozaki is devoutly religious and speaks in a formal, scriptural register. She declined to choose a hero name at first, later adopting "**Vine**." She is among the strongest fighters in Class 1-B — her vines have considerable reach and can restrain multiple opponents simultaneously — but she is reluctant to use force, preferring to end conflicts without injury.

Her personality is the series' clearest example of a character whose gentleness and combat capability coexist without contradiction.

---

### Juzo Honenuki (骨抜 柔造) — *Mudman*

**Quirk:** Softening · **Debut:** Chapter 32 / Season 2

Softens any non-living material he touches, turning ground, walls, and metal into quicksand-like substance — and can re-harden it.

Honenuki is a calm, tactical fighter and one of Class 1-B's most strategically valuable members. His Quirk controls the battlefield itself: he can trap opponents, create cover, or collapse structures. During the Joint Training Arc he leads Class 1-B's team in a match that Class 1-A nearly loses.

He is also a capable rescue specialist, since softening debris is one of the most useful Quirks for extracting trapped civilians.

## Remaining Students

### Kosei Tsuburaba (円場 硬化) — *Solid Air*
Creates solid walls and platforms of frozen air by exhaling. The walls' strength depends on his lung capacity. A defensive specialist who is often underestimated; his air prisons can immobilise opponents entirely. He is one of the class's more outspoken members and frequently argues with Monoma.

### Sen Kaibara (回原 旋) — *Spiral*
Rotates any part of his body at high speed, including his whole torso. The rotation generates drilling force and considerable momentum. A straightforward, energetic fighter who pairs well in team compositions.

### Kinoko Komori (小森 希乃子) — *Shemage*
Grows mushrooms from any surface she touches, including inside opponents' bodies through inhaled spores. Her Quirk is unsettling in combat and highly effective for area denial, blinding, and tracking. She is quiet and slightly eerie, in deliberate contrast to her cheerful appearance.

### Yui Kodai (小大 唯) — *Rule*
Alters the size of any non-living object she touches, up to a total mass limit. She can shrink obstacles or enlarge tools and equipment. Kodai communicates almost entirely through short phrases and gestures; she is extremely reserved.

### Nirengeki Shoda (庄田 二連撃) — *Mines*
Delivers a double impact — a second concussive force that strikes a target after his first, at range. An unusual Quirk that lets him hit from a distance without projectiles. He is polite and thoughtful, one of Class 1-B's more analytical members.

### Hiryu Rin (鱗 竜才) — *Long Weizi*
Grows and manipulates hard, sharp dragon-like scales across his body, which he can detach and fire. A Chinese exchange student. His scales provide both armour and ranged attack, making him one of the class's most complete fighters.

### Kojiro Bondo (凡戸 固次郎) — *Plamo*
Secretes a strong adhesive substance ("Cemedine," after the Japanese glue brand) from his body. Effective for immobilising opponents and sealing areas. His appearance — a face resembling a glue tube — makes him one of the class's most distinctive designs.

### Togaru Kamakiri (鎌切 尖) — *Jack Mantis*
Produces sharp, retractable blades from his arms and body, giving him a mantis-like fighting style. Aggressive and confident, he favours direct offense. He is one of Class 1-B's strongest close-combat fighters.

### Setsuna Tokage (取蔭 切奈) — *Lizardy*
Splits her body into up to fifty independently controlled pieces, each of which she can move telekinetically and reattach. Extraordinarily versatile for reconnaissance, restraint, and evasion. She is blunt, teasing, and one of the class's sharpest tacticians.

### Jurota Shishida (獅子目 獣太) — *Gevaudan*
Transforms into a beast-like form with enhanced strength, speed, and senses — and loses rational control while doing so. He speaks formally and behaves with old-fashioned courtesy, which makes his berserk transformation a deliberate contrast. His hero name references the Beast of Gévaudan.

### Shihai Kuroiro (黒色 支配) — *Vantablack*
Merges into and travels through darkness, emerging from any shadow. Extremely effective for stealth and ambush, but useless in bright light. He is theatrical and mischievous, and pairs naturally with Tokoyami's Dark Shadow — their Joint Training match is one of the arc's highlights.

### Reiko Yanagi (柳 霊子) — *Emily*
Manipulates objects telekinetically within a weight limit. Her pale appearance and deadpan delivery led classmates to compare her to a horror-film ghost, hence the hero name "Emily." She is quiet but formidable in team play.

### Pony Tsunotori (角取 ポニー) — *Rocketti*
Grows and fires her horns as projectiles, regrowing them afterwards. An American exchange student who speaks limited Japanese, which creates recurring comic misunderstandings. Her horns are strong enough to pierce concrete.

### Manga Fukidashi (吹出 漫我) — *Comicman*
Materialises onomatopoeia — he can draw sound-effect words into the air and make them physically real. "BOOM" explodes; "WIND" blows; "ICE" freezes. One of the most creatively potent Quirks in the series, limited mainly by his imagination and reaction time.

### Yosetsu Awase (泡瀬 洋雪) — *Welder*
Fuses any two objects he touches together at a molecular level. He can weld metal, attach opponents to surfaces, or join broken structures. During the Forest Training Camp he risks his life to protect Momo Yaoyorozu from a Nomu, welding it to the ground — one of Class 1-B's most heroic moments.

## Class 1-B as a Narrative Device

Class 1-B exists to make an argument. The series uses them to demonstrate that **Class 1-A's prominence is circumstantial, not intrinsic.** Monoma's constant complaint — that 1-A only gets attention because villains keep targeting them — is substantially correct, and the Joint Training Arc proves that Class 1-B can win.

Horikoshi gives Class 1-B comparatively little screen time but uses it precisely: they are the control group that shows what a normal hero class looks like when it is not at the centre of a national crisis.
