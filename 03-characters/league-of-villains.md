# Villains

*The League of Villains, the Meta Liberation Army, the Paranormal Liberation Front, and independent antagonists.*

> **Content note:** This page contains spoilers for events beyond Season 5, including the final arcs of the series.

---

## 1. The League of Villains

Founded by All For One and led by Tomura Shigaraki. Originally eight members, later merged with the Meta Liberation Army to form the **Paranormal Liberation Front**.

---

### Tomura Shigaraki (死柄木 弔) — *born Tenko Shimura (志村 転弧)*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Decay; later All For One |
| **Role** | Leader, League of Villains; Grand Commander, Paranormal Liberation Front |
| **Voice (JP)** | Koki Uchiyama; Arisa Sekine (child) |
| **Voice (EN)** | Eric Vale; Emily Fajardo (child) |
| **Debut** | Chapter 5 / Episode 9 |

The series' primary antagonist — and its most tragic character.

**Origin.** Tenko Shimura was the grandson of **Nana Shimura**, the seventh wielder of One For All and All Might's mentor. Terrified that All For One would target her bloodline, Nana sent her son away and cut off contact. Her son grew up hating her and hating hero society. He married, and had Tenko.

Tenko was a neglected, frightened child who wanted to be a hero. His father beat him for it. One day, his Quirk — **Decay** — awakened. He decayed his dog, then his mother, then his sisters, then his father, and then his entire neighbourhood. He ran into the street screaming for a hero to save him. **No one came.**

**All For One found him.** He gave the boy a new name — Tomura Shigaraki, "to mourn" — and raised him on hatred. Everything Shigaraki became was engineered.

**Quirk.** Decay disintegrates anything Shigaraki touches with all five fingers of one hand. It spreads through connected matter. Initially it required full five-finger contact; after his awakening in the Meta Liberation Army arc, it spreads uncontrollably through anything it touches and can no longer be stopped by removing contact. He compulsively scratches his neck when agitated — a self-soothing behaviour from childhood that eventually draws blood.

**Character.** Shigaraki begins as petulant and childish: he calls people "trash," throws tantrums, and disintegrates subordinates who annoy him. Over the series he becomes something far worse — calm, purposeful, and ideologically coherent. His stated goal is not conquest but **the destruction of hero society itself**, because he believes a world that produces people like him cannot be saved, only levelled.

The devastating irony of the series' structure: **All Might's mentor's grandson is the villain All Might spent his life fighting.** One For All and All For One have been warring across generations through a single family, and Nana Shimura's attempt to protect her bloodline is what destroyed it.

### All For One

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | All For One |
| **Role** | Founder and true leader of the League of Villains |
| **Voice (JP)** | Akio Otsuka; Hiroshi Kamiya (young) |
| **Voice (EN)** | John Swasey; Ryan Negrón (young) |
| **Debut** | Chapter 1 (mentioned) / Season 1 |

The oldest and most powerful villain in the series. All For One can **steal any Quirk from another person** and **transfer Quirks to others**. He has lived well over a century by accumulating life-extending and healing Quirks, and he wields dozens of stolen abilities simultaneously.

He is the direct mirror of One For All: where One For All is power freely given across generations, All For One is power taken from others and hoarded. He treats people as resources and Quirks as inventory.

**History.** In the era when Quirks first appeared, All For One ruled Japan's underworld. His younger brother, **Yoichi Shigaraki**, was born seemingly Quirkless — but had actually been *given* a stockpiling Quirk by his brother. That stockpile became **One For All**, and Yoichi used it to oppose him. All For One killed him. Every subsequent wielder has been fighting the same man.

He wounded All Might catastrophically in their first battle, which is why All Might's muscle form has a time limit. In the Kamino Ward battle (Season 3), All Might defeats him — and All For One is imprisoned in **Tartarus**. He is *smiling* as he is taken away, because the Symbol of Peace is gone.

**Later.** He escapes, recovers Shigaraki, and ultimately transfers his own Quirk into him, intending to overwrite Shigaraki's personality and use his body as a vessel. Whether this succeeds is one of the central questions of the series' final arcs.

### Toya Todoroki (轟 燈矢) — *Dabi*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Blueflame (Cremation) |
| **Voice (JP)** | Hiro Shimono; Ryoko Shiraishi (child) |
| **Voice (EN)** | Jason Liebrecht; Sara Ragsdale (child) |
| **Debut** | Season 3 |

Blue flames far hotter than Endeavor's. His body cannot withstand them: he inherited his mother's cold tolerance and his father's fire, so using his Quirk burns him. The purple staples and skin grafts across his body are the accumulated result.

**Identity.** Dabi is **Toya Todoroki** — Endeavor's eldest son. He trained desperately to earn his father's approval and was told he was too weak. He ran away, apparently died in a fire, and spent years planning to destroy Endeavor's reputation rather than his body.

He reveals himself in Season 5, Episode 107, immediately after Endeavor's victory over the High-End Nomu. His public broadcast — "the No. 1 Hero abused his children" — does more damage to hero society than any battle in the series.

**Character.** Dabi is nihilistic and cruel, but his cruelty is legible: he wants his father to feel what he felt. His relationship with **Himiko Toga** and **Twice** is the only genuine warmth he allows himself, and even that he frames as manipulation.

### Himiko Toga (渡我 被子)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Transform |
| **Voice (JP)** | Misato Fukuen |
| **Voice (EN)** | Leah Clark |
| **Debut** | Season 3 |

Drinks a person's blood and assumes their appearance, and — later — can use their Quirk. She fights with knives and is dangerously fast at close range.

**Origin.** Toga was a normal girl whose Quirk made her find blood beautiful. When she showed her parents, they were horrified and told her to suppress it. She was taken to therapy and instructed to become someone else. She eventually broke, killed a boy she loved, and fled.

She is the series' most explicit argument that **hero society manufactures its villains**. Nothing was wrong with Toga except that no one would let her be what she was.

**Character.** She is cheerful, affectionate, and completely without boundaries. She loves people by wanting to *become* them — she is obsessed with both Midoriya and Uraraka and considers them friends. Her final arc, in which she is confronted by Uraraka rather than fought, is the series' clearest statement that some villains should have been helped rather than defeated.

### Jin Bubaigawara (部階 晋) — *Twice*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Double |
| **Voice (JP)** | Daichi Endo |
| **Voice (EN)** | Newton Pittman |
| **Debut** | Season 3 |

Creates perfect duplicates of anything he can visualise and measure — objects, people, himself. Copies of living things decay after a period, but copies of Twice can create further copies, allowing exponential multiplication.

**Origin.** Twice formed a criminal gang. His comrades turned on each other, and he created copies of himself to survive — only to discover he could no longer tell which one was the original. He killed them all, including possibly himself. He is left permanently unsure whether he is the real Jin Bubaigawara.

**Character.** He wears a full-body suit and mask, speaks in contradictory double-talk ("**I love you all**" / "**Get away from me**"), and is the League's comic relief. He is also the emotional heart of the group. His devotion to Toga, Dabi, and Compress is the only thing that stabilises him.

**Death.** In Season 6, **Hawks** kills him while undercover — because Double is too dangerous to leave alive and because Twice was about to warn the Front of the impending raid. Twice dies happy, believing he had finally found real friends. It is the most morally complicated killing in the series.

### Atsuhiro Sako (迫 圧紘) — *Mr. Compress*

**Quirk:** Compress · **Voice (JP):** Tsuguo Mogami · **Voice (EN):** Kent Williams

Compresses anything within a circular area around his arm into a small marble, releasing it at will. He can compress people, buildings, or entire landscapes.

A showman descended from a famous thief. Mr. Compress treats villainy as performance and is the League's most composed member. He successfully kidnaps Bakugo during the Forest Training Camp by compressing him into a marble. He loses an arm during the Overhaul confrontation and later receives a prosthetic.

---

### Shuichi Iguchi (伊口 秀一) — *Spinner*

**Quirk:** Gecko · **Voice (JP):** Ryo Iwasaki · **Voice (EN):** Larry Brantley

A lizard mutation-type Quirk allowing him to cling to surfaces. He wields a large sword composed of many blades bound together.

Spinner joined the League because he admired **Stain** — he believes in Stain's critique of hero society and wants to see it corrected. He is the League's most ideologically motivated member and the character who most directly connects Stain's philosophy to Shigaraki's. As the series progresses he becomes increasingly radical, eventually leading the "near-meta" faction of the Paranormal Liberation Front.

---

### Kurogiri (黒霧) — *Warp Gate*

**Quirk:** Warp Gate · **Voice (JP):** Takahiro Fujiwara · **Voice (EN):** Chuck Huber

Creates teleportation portals from a dark mist that composes his body. He is the League's transport, barman, and de facto caretaker of Shigaraki.

**The truth.** Kurogiri is a **Nomu** constructed by Doctor Garaki from the corpse of **Oboro Shirakumo** — a U.A. student and close friend of Aizawa and Present Mic, who died young. His residual personality surfaces in Season 5 and becomes a major thread in Season 6, when Aizawa and Yamada reach the person buried inside him.

Later, All For One creates **Loud Cloud** as a separate entity, and Kurogiri's Quirk is distributed among the Front's lieutenants.

---

### Doctor Kyudai Garaki (殻木 久大) — *Daruma Ujiko*

**Quirk:** Life Force · **Voice (JP):** Minoru Inaba · **Voice (EN):** Mark Stoddard

All For One's physician and the creator of the **Nomu**. His Quirk doubles his vitality, which is why he has lived for over a century. He operates under numerous false identities and runs the laboratory that produces the Nomu and High-End Nomu.

He is responsible for Kurogiri, for Eri's captivity research, and for the escalation of Nomu technology that makes the Paranormal Liberation War possible.

---

### Gigantomachia

**Voice (JP):** Yasuhiro Mamiya · **Voice (EN):** Cris George

A colossal, near-indestructible creature created by Garaki from multiple Quirks, and fanatically loyal to All For One. He sleeps for most of the series and wakes to follow Shigaraki. His movement across Japan during the Paranormal Liberation War is one of the largest-scale threats in the story.

## 2. Independent and Affiliated Villains

### Chizome Akaguro (赤黒 血染) — *Stain, the Hero Killer*

**Quirk:** Bloodcurdle · **Voice (JP):** Go Inoue · **Voice (EN):** Robert McCollum

Paralyses anyone whose blood he ingests, for a duration proportional to blood type. A serial killer who murdered or crippled numerous Pro Heroes.

Stain is the series' first ideological antagonist. His thesis: modern heroes are celebrities motivated by fame and money, and only those willing to sacrifice themselves without reward are legitimate. He was once an admirer of All Might who became convinced that everyone who followed him had missed the point entirely. He crippled the Pro Hero **Ingenium** and murdered numerous others he judged to be fakes.

The narrative does not refute him entirely. Many Pro Heroes *are* motivated by money — Mt. Lady says so on camera. Stain's manifesto spreads after his arrest and radicalises Spinner, Muscular, and much of the subsequent villain population.

He is defeated by Midoriya, Todoroki, and Iida working together — and Stain spares Midoriya because he judges his motive pure.

---

### Kai Chisaki (廻 崎) — *Overhaul*

**Quirk:** Overhaul · **Voice (JP):** Kenjiro Tsuda · **Voice (EN):** Kellen Goff

Disassembles and reassembles anything he touches, including human bodies. He can heal himself by reassembling his own tissue, and kill by disassembling a person on contact.

Leader of the **Shie Hassaikai** yakuza group. He has severe mysophobia (fear of contamination) and wears gloves and a mask constantly.

**The abuse of Eri.** Overhaul discovered that **Eri's Rewind Quirk** could reverse a body to a prior state. He murdered her repeatedly and rewound her, harvesting her cells to manufacture **Quirk-Destroying Bullets** — weapons that permanently erase a person's Quirk. He told her she was cursed, that her existence hurt people, and that she should not have been born.

He is defeated by Midoriya at 1,000,000%. Afterwards, **Shigaraki** destroys both his arms — which, for a man whose Quirk requires touch, is a fate worse than death.

---

### Gentle Criminal (Danjuro Tobita) & La Brava (Manami Aiba)

| | Gentle | La Brava |
|---|---|---|
| **Quirk** | Elasticity | Love |
| **Voice (JP)** | Koichi Yamadera | Yui Horie |
| **Voice (EN)** | John Gremillion | Megan Shipman |

A pair of small-time villains who appear in the U.A. School Festival arc. **Gentle** failed the U.A. entrance exam twice, was expelled from hero society, and became a criminal purely for attention — he films his crimes and posts them online, where nobody watches.

**La Brava** loves him and can grant a one-time massive power boost to the person she loves most, once per day.

They are the series' gentlest villains. Midoriya defeats Gentle not by overpowering him but by telling him the truth: that his dream was never villainy. Gentle surrenders, and both are arrested. The arc exists to argue that hero society discards people who fail once, and that some villains are simply people who were never given a second chance.

---

### Kaina Tsutsumi (堤 河奈) — *Lady Nagant*

**Quirk:** Rifle · **Voice (JP):** Atsumi Tanezaki · **Voice (EN):** Erica Lindbeck

Her right arm transforms into a rifle, and she can curve her own bullets in flight with pinpoint accuracy. Formerly the **No. 3 Hero** and an assassin for the Hero Public Safety Commission.

She was ordered to kill people the Commission considered threats — including heroes. She broke under the moral weight, murdered the Commission's chairman, and was imprisoned in Tartarus. All For One frees her and uses her as a weapon against Midoriya.

Lady Nagant is the series' clearest indictment of the Commission itself: an institution that maintains peace by committing atrocities quietly, and then discards the people who carried them out.

## 3. The Meta Liberation Army

A secret organisation of over 100,000 members who believe Quirks should be used freely without government regulation. Founded historically by **Destro**; rebuilt in the modern era by his descendant.

The M.L.A. deliberately uses the retro terms "**Meta Abilities**" and "**superpowers**" rather than "Quirk," arguing that the word should not be used until liberation is achieved.

| **Re-Destro** | Rikiya Yotsubashi | Grand Commander; CEO of Detnerat | **Stress** — converts accumulated stress into size, strength, and durability | Hiroaki Hirata |
| **Geten** | — | Commander | **Ice** — creates and manipulates ice | Seiichiro Yamashita |
| **Trumpet** | Koku Hanabata | Commander; leader of the Hearts and Mind Party | **Incite** — amplifies the physical and mental abilities of his followers | Junji Majima |
| **Skeptic** | Tomoyasu Chikazoku | Commander; board member of Feel Good Inc. | **Anthropomorph** — converts people into data; master hacker | Tomokazu Sugita |
| **Curious** | Chitose Kizuki | Commander; executive director of Shoowaysha Publishing | **Landmine** — anything she touches becomes an explosive | Takako Honda |
| **Slidin' Go** | Tatsuyama Onoma | Pro Hero secretly working for the M.L.A. | **Overclock** — accelerates his own movements | — |

**Re-Destro vs. Shigaraki (Season 5).** The M.L.A. attempts to absorb or eliminate the League. Re-Destro beats Shigaraki comprehensively. In his darkest moment, Shigaraki has a vision of himself as Tenko Shimura and awakens Decay's true, unlimited form. He defeats Re-Destro.

Re-Destro — seeing in Shigaraki the perfect embodiment of liberation — **submits to him**. The M.L.A. merges with the League to form the **Paranormal Liberation Front**, with Shigaraki as Grand Commander and the former M.L.A. executives as lieutenants.

**Trivia.** The Meta Liberation Army is modelled on Marvel's Brotherhood of Mutants: an organisation of super-powered people demanding the right to exist without regulation.

## The Series' Argument About Villains

*My Hero Academia* is unusual in that nearly every major villain is a **product of hero society**:

- **Shigaraki** — a child who asked for help and received none
- **Dabi** — a son destroyed by his hero father's expectations
- **Toga** — a girl told her feelings were monstrous
- **Twice** — a man betrayed by people he trusted, left unsure whether he exists
- **Lady Nagant** — an agent broken by the institution she served
- **Gentle** — a man discarded for failing an exam twice
- **Stain** — a believer whose critique was correct enough to radicalise others
- **The Meta Liberation Army** — people who believe regulation itself is oppression

Horikoshi does not excuse any of them. They murder, torture, and destroy. But he insists on showing the causal chain — and in doing so makes the argument that a society which only celebrates the strong will keep producing the people who tear it down.
