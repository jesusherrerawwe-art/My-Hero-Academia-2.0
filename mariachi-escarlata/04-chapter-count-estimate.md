## The formula

- **Wattpad / light-novel sweet spot per chapter:** 2,000–2,500 words (one commute/read-before-bed session, ends on a hook).
- **Target length:** ~100,000–115,000 words → **48–52 numbered chapters + 1 prologue + 1 epilogue**, packaged as **3 volumes** of ~16–17 chapters each (proper light-novel feel; each volume ends on a climax).
- **Hard ceiling: 55.** Hard floor: 38 (emergency contest-word-limit cut → one volume, ~75k).
- **Canon compression law:** no canon arc gets more than **3 chapters**, and those chapters are Chuy-scenes only — what he sees, does, changes. We never retell a canon fight blow-by-blow. If a reader needs the full arc, the manga is free (Kevin's own rule).
- **Content ratio target:** ~60% original Chuy story / 40% canon refracted through him.
- **Short "Entreacto" interludes** (600–1,000 words, don't count toward the 50): cantina dream-debates with the Charro, Ana Sofía's "Contrato — Pruebas" notebook pages, Televisa/newspaper headlines, dad's voice-memo mariachi lessons. 5–6 total, used as pace-breathers.

## MEXICO NEVER LEAVES THE PAGE (Kevin's correction — structural rule)

The story moves to Japan; the story does NOT abandon Mexico. Mexico stays load-bearing through five channels:

1. **He carries it (always-on texture):** trumpet, sombrero, cape, jamoncillo, Duolingo, group chat with Escuadrón Cempasúchil, dad's voice memos, Ana Sofía's notebook pages, Antibacterial-wax care packages from mom. Immigrant thesis: home is something you pack.
2. **Mexico B-plot (interludes + video calls, ~1 beat per volume):** the legal clinic grows, CHG sends updates, Alebrije Inmortal looms, Mendoza rots in containment marked by the lien, El Alquimista talks. Interludes ("Entreactos") are partly set in Guadalajara — the shop, the back room, the clinic.
3. **Día de Muertos chapter (early November, Volume I/II border):** Chuy builds the ofrenda at Heights Alliance / Mexican consulate. Marigolds, sugar skulls, pan de muertos, photos — he explains a death culture that *welcomes* the dead to classmates who think death is only terror. The cape behaves strangely where the veil is thin; Eri sees her first sugar skull; the Charro is quiet that night ("*This is the one night I don't collect. They are already expected home.*"). Thematic keystone chapter.
4. **Winter break homecoming (~2 ch., Volume II→III bridge):** he flies home for Christmas/New Year. Dulcería, family, squad reunion, Ana Sofía's case files — AND the discovery that detonates the back half: **the Xolocristal pipeline now runs to Japan** (cartel internationalizing via Re-Destro's Detnerat supply chain / Giran's black market). Mexico's drug is in Japan's war. His two worlds collide because home followed him. Optional: a cartel figure (new, canon-free) crosses to Japan afterward as a pursuing sub-antagonist; Alebrije Inmortal gets a face-to-face scene.
5. **Act 3 requires Mexico:** the cantina threshold is in the Sierra Madre; Mictlán is Mexica cosmology — the descent opens where the pact was signed. Final stretch chapters return to Mexico (Sinaloa desert threshold, Guadalajara epilogue at the dulcería). Ana Sofía presents her case IN the dream-tribunal; family/squad appear in the ofrenda-assisted descent. The epilogue is in Guadalajara, under marigolds, with a song.

Budget check: ofrenda ch. (1) + winter homecoming (2) + Act 3 Mexico chapters (folded into the 5–6 descent ch.) = still inside the ~50 target; these replace, not add to, weaker canon-retelling slots.
