## PART 1 — ORIGIN (verbatim canon, first draft)

- **Full name:** Jesús Antonio Gutiérrez Reyes
- **Nicknames:** Chuy (family); Calavera, Huesitos (friends)
- **Hero name (later):** Mariachi Escarlata 🔥💀
- **Age:** 16 at U.A. transfer
- **Birthday:** March 2
- **Birthplace:** Colonia Americana, Guadalajara, Jalisco, México
- **Height:** 1.78 m; lean but broad-shouldered (from carrying candy sacks)
- **Tells:** hands in pockets when confident; polishes skull cracks with thumb when nervous

### The biological Quirk half (as observed)
- Classified **mutant-type**; manifested at **age 6 years 3 months** — late vs. global norm (~age 4)
- Two weeks of itching; then the skin around his entire skull **shed painlessly, bloodlessly**, revealing hyper-ossified bone:
  - Skull density **3.4× normal human**
  - Bone regeneration **12× faster** than normal
  - Only tissue remaining on the skull: delicate skin around green eyes, eyelids, tear ducts
  - No hair follicles on top; curly black tufts on the sides, trimmed by his mother
- **Bone-conduction hearing:** vibrations through bone — footsteps through a floor at 10 m; a heartbeat speeding up when someone lies; *later, the phantom hooves of El Charro Negro before anyone else hears them*
- No eyebrows or lips → strangers read him as angry when he's anxious; **his eyes do all the talking**
- Maintenance/drawbacks: antibacterial wax **2×/day**; cold winter air aches his cracks; **~3,200 calories/day** (pan dulce); low brass vibrations soothe fissures → trumpet from his father ("El Rey," "Cielito Lindo")

His **old black charro suit with gold embroidery is preserved in the closet**. Lesson: **"Un charro nunca le muestra miedo a su caballo."**
- **Mother — María Fernanda Reyes:** co-owner; jamoncillo, dulces de leche, borrachitos. Handmade him a **black lucha mask with red stitching so he could choose when to be seen**; sews soft linings into high collars. Created **"jamoncillo de calavera"** (skull-shaped, red chili-powder cracks) as a joke **after Sinaloa** *(= after the Part 2 ambush, now resolved)*; shop bestseller.
- **Sister — Ana Sofía Gutiérrez Reyes (19):** Quirkless law student, Universidad de Guadalajara, top 5%. Thesis: *"Reforma de la Ley de Imagen Heroica: El caso del anonimato forzado."* Speech coach (formal speech when nervous); notebook **"Contrato — Pruebas"** recording the Charro dreams; free legal clinic for mutant-type kids facing school discrimination.

### Childhood, masks, San Juan de Dios
- Ages 6–8 acute isolation; four masks: Blue Demon replica (6); mom's handmade black/red (primary through age 10, still kept as treasure); training mask; cheap blue market mask.

## PART 2 — THE PACT WITH EL CHARRO NEGRO (EXPANDED) (verbatim canon)

### Setup: the Sinaloa field trip (age 14, second year at CHG)
- Joint training field trip, **Sierra Madre Occidental, Sinaloa**; routine 3-day survival/squad-coordination exercise.
- Four escuadrones, **24 students, 3 instructors, 2 regional Pro Heroes**. Alebrije Inmortal absent (central base).
- Chuy's squad: **Escuadrón Cempasúchil**; named squadmate **Torres**.

### The Ambush (~midnight)
- Explosion destroys the comms relay; Quirk-based signal jammer kills radio/cell/satellite.
- **"Ojo de Diablo" Mendoza** emerges with 6–8 armed henchmen. CHG intel later: he believed the camp was a **military observation post tracking his Xolocristal smuggling routes** — not after students.
- Systematic assault; two instructors incapacitated early; students separated; Escuadrón Cempasúchil forms a defensive circle around first-years.

### The Turning Point
- Mendoza, pressured/paranoid, ingests a **refined Xolocristal dose**; corrosive output triples; lines break; Pros overwhelmed.
- Chuy does his signature move — yells **"¡Yo voy primero!"** and charges, skull as battering ram.
- Mendoza swats him aside with an amplified blast **without looking**. Chuy crashes into a pine: **fractured ribs, concussion, unable to move**, combat-ineffective on the damp forest floor.

### The Despair
- Hears Torres screaming his name, younger students crying; Alebrije estimated **22 minutes away**. Later describes it as *"the same feeling as when I was 6 and couldn't stop kids from staring — absolute impotence."*
- The world goes muffled, underwater-quiet. He hears **hooves on stone in a place with no stone**; cold draft smelling of **copal, old leather, desert dust**.
- A **~1.90 m charro silhouette**: sombrero shadowing the face except **two red-glowing eyes like coals**; impeccable black suit with **silver embroidery that absorbs light**; behind him, faintly, a **huge black horse with eyes like fire, snorting steam**.
- **This is El Charro Negro.**
- **AU nature of the entity:** not a campfire villain — a **supernatural vigilante/collector**. After the colonial devil's pact and curse, **Mictlán claimed him and employed him**: hunt and collect Earth's most corrupt, greedy souls and guide them to Mictlán for judgment. He is **not ruler of Mictlán** (that is **Mictlantecuhtli and Mictecacíhuatl**) — he is *their charro, their debt collector; a necessary balance.*
- Since Quirks emerged, wicked souls **tripled** (Quirk cartel bosses, Xolocristal traffickers, exploiting influencers). Overwhelmed alone, he needed **eyes in the mortal realm — a messenger**.
- He asks, in a tired, centuries-old voice: **"¿Darías todo por salvarlos?"**
- **Chuy doesn't hesitate. He says yes.**

### The Awakening
- **Pitch-black and crimson flames** erupt from his back/shoulders "like a hellish cape born from his fissures." The fire doesn't burn forest or him: **cold to good people, heavy to rotten ones.**
- Flames lift him into the air; **green eyes burn red**; the skull's red-black cracks flare like lava; he is **pulled by the cape at blinding speed**, not running.
- Berserk, furious battle with Mendoza; trees snap; **ground cracks under the weight of cursed gold**.
- His subconscious keeps classmates safe, redirecting Mendoza's blasts away from cabins — CHG instructors' later "proof Chuy was still in there."
- **Mendoza falls unconscious, "hollowed."** Chuy collapses from exhaustion.

### Aftermath and Limbo
- **Alebrije Inmortal arrives ~11 minutes later** with the Mexican Special Forces Hero Unit; Mendoza secured; camp evacuated; Chuy rushed to **Hospital Civil de Culiacán**; **unconscious 36 hours**; **no Xolocristal in his system**.
- Mendoza in containment, **"hollowed": alive but Quirkless for 24 hours, with no memory of why he was greedy**.
- Chuy's consciousness wakes instead in **a dilapidated, dusty cantina in a barren desert under a starless sky**; one bare swinging bulb; dust-covered tables; smell of old wood and mezcal. The Charro sits across, tilting his sombrero back.
- "Who are you?" → **"Names don't matter where I come from. Only debts."**
- The power belongs to the Charro; they have **bound a contract**. Chuy is his messenger/eyes in the mortal realm. The Charro **watched him for a while** — chosen for his unyielding, desperate urge to save others at any cost, even himself. The Charro frames it as **passing down his power, "much like All Might passing One For All to Deku."**

### The Ultimatum
- **By Chuy's 18th birthday, deliver at least ten corrupt souls ("almas podridas") as tribute to Mictlán** — people whose greed/corruption caused severe harm, not common criminals; **the flames themselves judge worthiness.**
- Mendoza's soul was **claimed during the fight → 1 of 10; nine remaining** ("Te quedan nueve, Chuy.").
- Failure (or refusal): the Charro claims **Chuy's own soul** — he wanders Mictlán for eternity **as the next Charro Negro**.

### The Wake-up Call
- Before Chuy can answer, he wakes in the real hospital bed: worried Escuadrón Cempasúchil classmates, **five missed calls from José Luis**, and a **physical cape folded at the bed's foot — black outside, crimson inside, warm to the touch, faintly sounding of distant hooves in complete silence. Forged from Mictlán itself.**
- Since that night: **recurring dream-visions of the Charro**; he counts down the remaining souls; the two debate heroism, redemption, and **who holds the authority to decide who is saved** — **Chuy believes in second chances; the Charro does not. This debate is the core of his U.A. arc.**

## PART 3 — GROWTH, FAMILY, AND THE ROAD TO U.A. (verbatim canon, ages 14→16)

### First days wearing the cape (Hospital Civil de Culiacán)
- First thing he sees: the cape. He tries to remove it — **cannot; it is warm, slightly fused to his back fissures "like roots into bone."** CHG Support later confirms: **not fabric — condensed Mictlán flame in solid form; cannot be removed by force.**
  - Retracts into his back when calm; full charro cape manifests when **heart rate > 110 bpm** (involuntary).
- Day 1–2: refuses sleep; hears hooves in the hallway; asks nurses *"¿Cuántos me quedan?"* ("How many do I have left?"); squadmate **Torres** stays; stares at his own hands as not his.
- Day 3: **first involuntary good deed** — a boy in the next bed chokes on water; the cape shoots out 2 m uncommanded and **gently pats his back** until he coughs it up; child lives; cape retracts; Chuy cries for an hour: *"it moved without me, half-like it knew someone needed help before I did."*
- Day 5: discharged. Father drives **10 hours** Guadalajara→Culiacán; asks nothing; places his old trumpet in the passenger seat and plays **"Cielito Lindo"** the entire drive; Chuy sleeps for the first time since the pact, cape tucked around him like a blanket. **Trumpet = safety ever after.**

### CHG status change
- Mandatory psych leave 1 month → returned day 4: *"If I stop, the voice in the bar gets louder."*
- Nicknames: **Punto de Brecha** ("Breach Point," pre-Sinaloa) → **"El de la Capa"** ("Cape Guy," post-Sinaloa); go-to against Xolocristal users.
- Fissures crept from jawline → **base of neck**, branching root-like; high-collared shirts + mom's linings; **20-minute morning skull-polishing ritual** — *"lustrar mis botas, pero soy yo"* ("polishing my boots, except I'm the boots").
- **Grades:** Rescate A→S (auto-shield rescues); Combate Cuerpo a Cuerpo A-→A; Control de Quirk B→C+ (two Quirks, one semi-sentient); Trabajo en Equipo S→S+ (**highest teamwork score in CHG second-year history**); Teoría Heroica A.
- Deliberately loses spars by removing the cape to his locker and fighting skull-only: *"If I only know how to win when I'm a powerhouse, I'm not learning."* Instructor: "That's when I knew he was ready for U.A."
- Squad tells: **hands in pockets + chest out** = calm, skull-only, letting others shine; **hands out + cape flare** = real, everyone gets behind him; **eyes dart left** = lying (his only readable tell).
- **Techniques (as of CHG):**
  - **Llama Fría** ("Cold Flame") — drains a target's **Quirk stamina** instead of dealing physical damage; walks through corrosive blasts; perfect counter to overclocked Xolocristal users.
  - **Peso del Oro Maldito** ("Weight of the Cursed Gold") — pins/weighs villains down.
  - **Rebozo de Almas** ("Shawl of Souls") — dome shield wrapping civilians; those inside hear a **muffled trumpet lullaby** (rebozo = the cloth Mexican mothers wrap/carry children in).
  - **Cabezazo "El Toro"** ("The Bull" headbutt) — skull + cape weight combined; impacts ring like a bell.
- Thursday routine: Escuadrón Cempasúchil homework in the dulcería back room; José Luis free borrachitos; mariachi lesson: a mariachi **keeps singing even if his trumpet breaks**。

### Family rises to the curse
- **María Fernanda:** jamoncillo de calavera bestseller; linings; never asks directly — *"Si te da frío, te pones la capa, mijo. Si te da calor, te la quitas. Pero no me la dejes tirada en el piso."* ("Cold, wear the cape; hot, take it off — just don't leave it on the floor.")
- **José Luis:** quit his part-time taxi gig to run the shop full-time (home for nightmares); repaired his old black/gold charro suit and **hung it in Chuy's room without a word** → understood: *"When you earn it, you wear it."* Teaches the **tilted sombrero** (not straight) and a proper **grito**.
- **Ana Sofía:** used law contacts to classify the cape **"Culturally Significant Support Item"** so the state cannot confiscate it as an unknown Quirk artifact; only person who knows the full count + deadline; advice: **"If you have to deliver souls, deliver them your way. Document everything, so you can prove they were truly corrupt."**

### The Talk (age 14 years 8 months, after closing)
Chuy confesses everything (cantina, Charro, 10 souls by 18, Mendoza = 1, 9 left, failure = eternal wandering). José Luis was already peeling almonds for jamoncillo:
- *"Yo fui mariachi 10 años. Sé cuando alguien está cantando con otra voz... tu grito no es tu grito. A veces es más viejo. Más cansado."* ("I was a mariachi for 10 years. I know when someone is singing with another voice. Since you came back, your grito isn't yours sometimes — older, more tired.")
- After the confession, hand on Chuy's skull (the family's most intimate gesture): **"Tú no eres su cobrador, Chuy. Tú eres su mensajero. Un mensajero puede decidir cómo entrega el mensaje."** ("You're not his collector. You're his messenger. A messenger gets to decide how the message is delivered.")
  - Confirmed Mendoza sold Xolocristal to kids; dad's verdict: collect the other nine the same way — **but your way**.
- Puts his old charro sombrero on Chuy's skull; it's too big, falls over his eyes; **both laugh — his first real laugh since Sinaloa.**
- CHG medical: fissures **stopped spreading for two weeks** after paternal validation; psych note: framing as messenger-not-collector restored agency.

## PART 4 — POWERS (verbatim canon, final powers dossier)

Two Quirks in one body: one born with (shield), one given (burden).

### PART A — Biological: CALAVERA (always active)
- **Cráneo Reforzado (Reinforced Skull):** headbutts through 15 cm concrete (wall cracks, he doesn't); shatters knife blades; frontal plate withstands **800 PSI** without the plate cracking; CHG tested doors, lockers, a car hood. Weak points: **back of head and jaw are weaker**; **5–6 hard headbutts in quick succession → bell-rung dizziness, must sit down** (brain still takes acceleration; concussions possible per Sinaloa).
- **Resonancia Ósea (Bone Conduction):** footsteps through floors/walls within 10 m; heartbeat-spike lie detection (requires concentration + quiet); hears the Charro's hooves first; identifies classmates by step vibration in the dorm hall; hates nights (hears everyone tossing and turning).
- **Intimidación Natural (not a Quirk):** civilians/low villains freeze 1–2 seconds on first close-up sight of him in the dark; squad tactic (Chuy steps out, villains flinch, squad moves); **Chuy hates using it** (bullying echo).
- Limits/life-logistics: zero ranged offense; unreadable face (no brows/lips); eyes dart left when lying; 3,200 cal/day — pan dulce and **tortas ahogadas**; winter cold aches the fissures → mother-knit scarves; 20-min antibacterial wax ritual, *"lustrar mis botas, pero soy yo."*

### PART B — Mythological: LLAMA DE MICTLÁN / CAPA DEL CHARRO (gained at 14)
Not fire — **soul-flames from Mictlán**; they burn corruption, greed, and Quirk energy, not skin. Good people/civilians/classmates feel cold wind; the truly corrupt (harmed others for profit) feel ~100 kg of cursed gold. Visual: pitch-black flame with crimson edges, like burning copal, forming the cape; emotional stress makes fissures glow/leak flame.

**Core techniques:**
- **Capa del Mictlán:** part of him/part separate; glide 8–10 s by cape-pulling; autonomous shield vs bullets/blades/emitter blasts for allies within 3 m even unseen; extends 5 m to catch falling civilians; physical cape persists with flames off — warm, never wet/dirty, faint hoof-sound in silence, irremovable, retracts into back. UA Support tried to analyze it; **Hatsume: "It smells like old church and it's judging me."**
- **Peso del Oro Maldito (Weight of the Cursed Gold):** thrown flame-ball; on a judged-corrupt target it solidifies and pins 30 s–2 min by corruption depth; sensation = buried alive in coins; on innocents = cold wind only. Judgment belongs to the flames, preventing abuse.
- **Llama Fría (Cold Burn):** drains **Quirk stamina**: −20–30% output for 5 min, marathon exhaustion; capture-without-killing; no effect on the good.
- **Ojos del Mictlán (Mictlán Eyes):** green→red while active; sees **"manchas" (soul-stains)** on the severely corrupt within 15 m; nothing on normal people or petty criminals; overuse → nosebleed + stains visible on everyone for ~1 h (paranoia; sit in the dark).

**Combined moves:**
- **Cabezazo de Brecha "El Toro":** cape wrapped around skull as battering ram — weight + guard + fear; through reinforced doors/walls; the 7-strike Barranca feat.
- **Rebozo de Almas (Shawl of Souls):** 4 m dome shield; inside, muffled mariachi trumpet (the Culiacán-drive lullaby) calms civilians/children; outside, the flames look terrifying.

**Ultimate moves (MHA-style, culture-named):**
1. **Serenata del Mictlán:** forehead slams ground; 10 m shockwave/flame ring pins all corrupt targets with Peso at once; allies in range get a warm stamina-restoring pulse; cost — 15 s dizziness, fissures bleed light, forced kneel.
2. **Grito de El Charro Negro:** lets the Charro take **10% control**; a guttural mariachi grito, flame-amplified, with phantom bell and hoof toll; within 20 m low villains drop weapons in fear, corrupt villains hear their victims' voices for 3 s and freeze; cost — **Chuy remembers nothing he said; the Charro speaks through him in old Spanish; it terrifies Chuy; afterward he asks, "What did I say?"**
3. **El Último Mariachi (WIP, unmastered at 16):** cape detaches into a 3× giant **charro silhouette** that mimics his movements (punch/grab/throw), 12 s max at 16; cost — the soul count flickers in his vision, he hears *"Te quedan ocho…"* without a claim, the Charro grows louder, fissures spread faster.

**Final Tribute — Soul Claim (not an attack):** defeated judged villain is wrapped by the cape; black-crimson light, silence like copal smoke; villain falls alive, "hollowed" — Quirkless 24 h, emptied of the memory of their greed, cold. The soul is delivered to Mictlán for Mictlantecuhtli/Mictecacíhuatl's judgment, not destroyed. **Chuy doesn't kill; he delivers. Count at transfer: 2/10; 8 left by age 18.**

### Training vs villain split (intentional balance)
- Vs classmates/training: cape set aside, skull only — elite defense, zero offense, loses **7/10**; classroom underdog until the first real villain attack.
- Vs corrupt cartel villains: skull + cape = powerhouse who can solo threats that give Todoroki/Bakugo trouble (flames punish greed — what real villains run on).

### The Bakugo Anomaly
- Cape reads cold around Deku, Uraraka, Iida, Todoroki, Tsuyu. Bakugo is the exception for the first 2–3 weeks: not black-claimable, not clean — faint **grey stain**, low heat, crimson sparks, wet-serape heaviness. Chuy panics and pulls away; Bakugo thinks he's being feared and fumes (*"THE HELL ARE YOU STARING AT, EXTRA SKULL?!"*). Aizawa/All Might explain: the flames judge **greed that harms others for profit**, not loud ego — *"he's not rotten, he's just… Bakugo."* The cape learns his pattern and goes cold; mutual respect begins.

### Costs & weaknesses
- Countdown: 8 souls by 18; insomnia, anxiety, cantina visions.
- **Fissure spread:** heavy use advances the red-black cracks ~1 mm down the neck toward the **heart** (CHG doctors don't know the endpoint).
- Charro's voice: dream-debates — *"¿Quién eres tú para decidir quién merece redención, Chuy?"*
- Soul exhaustion after big uses: can't feel his heartbeat, numb hands, sugar + rest required; **Recovery Girl: the soul is tired, not the body — she can't heal it.**
- Cape autonomy (protecting unseen people; Aizawa: *"partially sentient — train like Tokoyami"*).

### Fight style & stats (16)
Mid-range **controller/tank** — breach, area denial, capture; walks in hands-in-pockets, cape billowing, out-endures villains under gold weight. Motto: *"El charro no grita cuando tiene miedo. Canta más fuerte."*
- Power A (flames) / B− (skull) · Speed B (cape-pull) / C (on foot) · Technique B+ · Intelligence A− (strategic, not book-smart) · Cooperativeness S (highest) · **Flare/Dramatic Entrance: S+ ("obviously, he's a mariachi").**

## HANA'S ANALYSIS — PARTS 1–2

### The structural engine
1. **The pact inverts the legend.** Folk Charro Negro targets *greed*; this one chose Chuy for *selfless love* — "¿Darías todo por salvarlos?" The myth's recruitment language (the Charro needs a replacement to rest) is intact, but the bait is sainthood, not gold. A charity trap: the kindest possible devil's bargain. Chuy's "yes" is the most in-character and most devastating thing he could have said.
2. **Dark mirror of One For All (made explicit in-text).** OFA: power passed to save people, price borne voluntarily, lineage of holders; Chuy's inheritance: power passed to *collect* people, quota enforced, **failure turns him into the previous holder forever.** Deku's legacy says "save everyone"; Chuy's says "judge ten." Same gesture (a dying/immortal figure passes power to a selfless green-eyed boy), opposite morality. Their friendship is a theological argument neither can walk away from.
3. **The ticking clock = series spine.** Age 14 pact → 18 deadline → 10 souls, 1 already claimed (9 remain) → transfer at 16 = ~2 years, which maps onto a 3-year UA school story with built-in finale pressure. Every arc can quietly advance, delay, or complicate the count.
4. **Chuy vs. Stain (and the HPSC).** "Flames judge corrupt souls; second chances don't exist" is *Stain's ideology with divine paperwork*, and it also literalizes the HPSC's assassination/Tartarus debt-collector role (Lady Nagant). Chuy's "I believe in second chances" is his daily refusal of his own power's morality — his conflict isn't external villains, it's his cape agreeing with the world's harshest people.
5. **Mictlán canon accuracy is doing heavy lifting.** Mictlantecuhtli/Mictecacíhuatl as his divine employers; the soul journey through Mictlán (myth: four years, nine trials, guided by Xoloitzcuintlis) — **Xolocristal is named for the dog that guides the dead, and its top trafficker was the first soul guided to Mictlán.** That symmetry is absurdly good. The nine remaining souls + nine levels of Mictlán is a possible finale blueprint.
6. **The relic is the costume.** The cape folded at the hospital (black/crimson, warm, hoof-sounds in silence) = the cape in the concept art, forged in Mictlán. It will combine with father's preserved charro suit and mother's stitching: family + underworld in one hero costume. Customs/support-course comedy built in.
7. **Berserk-mode safeguard is emotional, not just physical.** Green→red eyes, cape pulls him, gold-cracks ground; his protection of classmates is the proof-of-humanity test (echoes Eri, Dark Shadow's berserk Dark Shadow, Naruto's fox cloak) — the "is he still in there?" scene gives every ally a detection method and every future loss-of-control its horror.
8. **Powerlessness-first sequencing.** He loses first — swatted "without looking," ribs, concussion, the 22-minute eternity — and the power answers impotence, not ambition. The origin rhymes with the age-6 staring impotence (explicit in-text) and the San Juan de Dios shield: every time he can't protect, the story escalates the cost of being able to.
9. **Consent flaw = Ana Sofía's case.** The contract was formed at 14, under mortal duress, with **terms disclosed only after acceptance** (ultimatum delivered "as Chuy prepared to accept," then he wakes before responding) — unenforceable by any mortal law: minority, duress, no informed consent, no consideration disclosed. The Charro's cosmic law doesn't care. "Contrato — Pruebas" is literally discovery for a trial against a death god. The family's three disciplines all weaponize against the curse (father: don't fear the horse; mother: control visibility; sister: break the fine print).
10. **Aftermath craft:** no Xolocristal in his blood gives investigators a genuinely unexplainable case file; 36 h coma and time-dilated cantina dream = the first on-record supernatural event in a science-first hero world; five missed calls from dad is one quiet image that says everything about the family.
