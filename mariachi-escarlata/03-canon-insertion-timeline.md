# Canon Insertion Timeline — Before & After Kamino

*Prepared by H.A.N.A. for the Mariachi Escarlata project. All episode/chapter data taken from the audited repo encyclopedia (`02-seasons/`), so it matches the corrected canon.*

**Insertion point (locked in Chuy's Part 3):** **One week after the Kamino Incident — the evening Class 1-A has just moved into Heights Alliance dorms.** Canonically this sits **between episodes 50 and 51 of Season 3** (in-universe: late August, start of the second semester). Aizawa announces him in the 1-A common room; he physically arrives the next day.

---

## PHASE 0 — THE WORLD CHUY WALKS INTO (everything before his arrival)

### Season 1 (eps. 1–13) — U.A. Beginnings
- **Entrance Exam (1–3):** Quirkless Deku meets a weakened All Might, inherits One For All, scores 0 villain points / 60 rescue points and is admitted. Bakugo scores 77 villain points.
- **Quirk Apprehension Test (4):** Aizawa threatens to expel the lowest scorer; Deku proves himself with one controlled OFA flick.
- **Battle Trial (5–8):** Deku vs. Bakugo indoor training; Iida is named MVP; hero costumes debut.
- **U.S.J. (9–13):** the League of Villains' first attack; Kurogiri warps the class into zones; the Nomu beats Aizawa (his shattered arm/facial fractures happen HERE, not later); All Might defeats the Nomu with **Detroit Smash**. Shigaraki and Kurogiri retreat.

### Season 2 (eps. 14–38)
- **Sports Festival (14–25):** cavalry battle (15-min limit; Deku breaks his own finger to shake Shinso's brainwash); tournament; Todoroki's "it's your power" moment; final podium Bakugo (chained, furious), Todoroki, Tokoyami/Iida. Hero names chosen at ep. 26 (Bakugo's King/Lord Explosion Murder rejected; Iida registers his real name and declares "Ingenium" in the field against Stain).
- **Vs. Hero Killer / Hosu (26–33):** Iida's brother Tensei is crippled by the Hero Killer Stain; Deku, Shoto and Iida fight Stain; Stain is captured alive and sent to **Tartarus** (Native survives). Dabi and Toga are first glimpsed.
- **Final Exams (34–38):** students fight teachers in pairs; five fail practicals (but everyone still goes to the summer camp). Ep. 38 = Deku's mall encounter with Shigaraki (NOT the OFA reveal).

### Season 3, first half (eps. 39–49) — the road to Kamino
- **Forest Training Camp (39–45):** summer camp with the Pussycats; the Vanguard Action Squad attacks at night (Dabi, Toga, Twice, Mr. Compress, Spinner, Muscular, Moonfish, Mustard, Magne). Aizawa takes only minor injuries; Ragdoll is kidnapped and later left Quirkless (AFO steals Search). **Bakugo is captured** by Mr. Compress at ep. 45. Tokoyami's Dark Shadow goes berserk in the dark and kills Moonfish's threat level (Moonfish is restrained).
- **Hideout Raid / Kamino (46–49):** police + Pros raid the League's Kamino Ward hideout. Five students — **Deku, Iida, Todoroki, Kirishima, Yaoyorozu** — disobey orders and mount their own rescue. AFO intervenes, reveals he killed All Might's mentor Nana Shimura, and tries to take Bakugo. All Might arrives on his last embers of OFA and defeats AFO with **United States of Smash** (ep. 48–49). On **live national television**, his muscle form fails: the Symbol of Peace deflates into gaunt, coughing Toshinori Yagi. AFO is sent to **Tartarus** (where Stain already is). Bakugo is rescued.
- **Off-screen roundup:** Kurogiri is captured in the post-camp/Kamino aftermath by Gran Torino and Tsukauchi (his identity as Oboro Shirakumo stays secret until ep. 107). The surviving League core — Shigaraki, Dabi, Toga, Twice, Compress, Spinner — goes into hiding, broke and scattered.

### Episode 50 — "End of the Beginning, Beginning of the End" (the week Chuy arrives)
- All Might **retires permanently** and joins U.A. faculty as a hero-basics teacher.
- The media and public blame **U.A. — and specifically Bakugo — for the Symbol's retirement** (the narrative: the League targeted him and All Might burned out saving him).
- U.A. announces on-campus housing for safety; **Heights Alliance dorms** are readied; Class 1-A moves in (ep. 51).
- The mood is heavy: the five rescue students carry guilt for disobeying; Bakugo carries public guilt and private suspicion; Deku knows All Might's flame is out; the country is visibly anxious without a Symbol.

> **In-universe date feel:** late August / very start of September. Chuy's Part 3 common-room scene and his first night in the dorm happen during exactly this window.

- **Dark Deku arc (post-war eps):** Deku drops out of UA to hunt villains alone, gaunt and black-caped — the school's darkest section; 1-A comes to bring him home.

### Seasons 7–8 (eps. 139–171)
- S7: the U.A. Traitor reveal (Aoyama), Star and Stripe vs. Shigaraki, the final war buildup, civilians sheltering at U.A.
- S8: the Final War — Deku vs Shigaraki/AFO, Bakugo's death and revival, Toga's death (ch. 395/ep. 169) saving Uraraka, Dabi/Endeavor resolution; AFO defeated. Ch. 430 ends the weekly manga; **ch. 431 "More"** is the eight-years-later epilogue (anime ep. 171, May 2, 2026).
- **Timeline math for Chuy:** he arrives at 16; his 18th-birthday deadline is ~19 months later ≈ **March of third year**, which lands AFTER the canon final war. The canon war is Act Two; the **Mictlán/contract finale is original Act Three** — no manga can spoil it.

## STATE OF THE CAST THE SECOND HE WALKS IN (cheat sheet)

- **Deku:** still has All Might; secretly knows the embers are dying; post-rescue guilt; Full Cowl user, Shoot Style not yet developed; OFA secrets fully his own.
- **Bakugo:** publicly blamed for All Might's retirement; privately exploding with suspicion about Deku's power (Ground Beta is ~weeks away); will be told the OFA truth at eps. 60–61.
- **Todoroki:** license exam next; his family arc (and Inasa rivalry) about to open.
- **Iida, Kirishima, Yaoyorozu:** the other three Kamino rescue-five; carry disobedience guilt.
- **Aizawa:** minor cuts/bruises only (NO eyepatch/leg loss yet — that's the PLW), exhausted, running the dorm restructuring; becomes Chuy's homeroom teacher.
- **All Might:** now depowered, gaunt Toshinori, teaching hero basics at UA.
- **Vlad King / 1-B:** across the dorm building; Joint Training months away.
- **Hatsume Mei:** Support Course, available from the ultimate-move training sessions onward.
- **Jiro:** in 1-A, festival band arc ~six months out.
- **Shinso:** not yet in the hero course; appears at the license-era and Joint Training.
- **The League:** underground, broke, at six surviving core members; Kurogiri gone; AFO in Tartarus; they don't yet know Chuy exists.
- **Stain, AFO:** both in Tartarus (until the S6 breakout).
- **Eri, Overhaul, the Big Three, Hawks, Mirko:** none of them have appeared yet.
